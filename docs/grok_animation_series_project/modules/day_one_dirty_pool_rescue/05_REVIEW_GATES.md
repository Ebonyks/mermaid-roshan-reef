# Blocking review gates

Do not generate a dependent shot until its controlling anchor/previous shot is
accepted. Mark every attempt `APPROVED`, `REVIEW` or `REJECT` with a reason.

## Gate 1 — room geography

- 16:9 complete room; no crop or mirror.
- Huge oval pool and pearl foreground rim remain dominant.
- Shell lounge left; rainbow waterfall left-of-center.
- Reef arch center-right; seahorse right-of-center; towel shelf far right.
- Flower float left of center; star float right of center.
- No new doorway, throne, window, fountain, furniture or landmark.

## Gate 2 — dirt style and state

- Four readable problems exist at the opening: surface, waterfall, rim,
  seahorse.
- Dirt is matte, broad, calm and child-safe—not glossy PBR, haloed or
  micro-detailed.
- Dirt state decreases only in order `4 → 3 → 2 → 1 → 0`.
- A removed cluster never returns, migrates or changes into a new problem.
- Room is neglected but not frightening, filthy, toxic or sewage-like.

## Gate 3 — seahorse identity and rescue

- Same established lavender/aqua fountain, long snout, round eye, scalloped
  crest, cream segmented belly, curled tail, coral base and pearl pedestal.
- No horn, eyelashes, jewelry, extra fin/limb or animal redesign.
- One soggy pink wrapper is clearly lodged sideways in the mouth/nozzle before
  rescue and reads as trash, not tongue, wound, food or body part.
- In `SH060`, the wrapper visibly transfers to Roshan's right hand and the
  nozzle becomes clear. No teleportation or fused fingers.
- Seahorse is magically tired/relieved, never injured or terrified.

## Gate 4 — Roshan identity and acting

- Correct approved child face, age, tiara, hair, outfit, palette and mer-tail.
- Exactly two arms/hands and one mer-tail; no legs, feet or shoes.
- Concern remains gentle and readable; no crying, panic or trauma.
- One clear cleanup action per shot with believable hand/object ownership.
- Roshan remains lower-left/inward-facing unless an authored cut says otherwise.

## Gate 5 — Rumi identity and entrance

- Rumi is absent before the end of room restoration.
- Exact approved young-adult face, pointed ears and strong brows.
- Enormous sculptural violet braided high ponytail with tied loose tip.
- Star-shell earrings; lavender/navy gold-trim sea-jacket; white high-neck shell
  top; three-shell clasp.
- Turquoise/deep-aqua tail through lavender to broad coral-pink split fin, plus
  two coral side fins.
- Exactly two arms/hands and one tail. No legs, feet, shoes, wings or weapon.
- Rise uses slow confident swim, braid/sleeve lag, upright settle and one broad
  wave. No liquid morphing or continuous frantic motion.
- Never resembles or cites the rejected unrelated `rumi_violet` candidate.

## Gate 6 — light, water and effects

- Initial olive/grey wash never obscures faces or geography.
- Light and clarity improve monotonically after each cleanup.
- Final waterfall is the exact ordered rainbow shell-waterfall.
- Final seahorse stream is clean and graceful.
- Effects are sparse: one broad ripple, restrained pearl glints, few prismatic
  points. No bloom storm, fog wall or effects covering acting.

## Gate 7 — style and full-frame delivery

- Matches approved base-video painted-cel style and Series Style Bible.
- Complete newly generated frames; no pasted runtime sprites, chroma keys,
  masks, morphs, optical flow, tweened cutouts or static-layer camera tricks.
- Stable anatomy, materials and linework throughout motion.
- No photorealism, 3D volume, plastic mobile-game gloss, webtoon/vector style,
  hard uniform black ink or texture crawling.
- No text, UI, pointers, touch buttons, subtitles or watermark removal.

## Gate 8 — continuity and child audience

- Start state matches accepted previous ending frame for continuous action.
- Screen direction, eyelines, object ownership and emotional beat are correct.
- Room landmarks never move between shots.
- Roshan remains safe and in control; no fail state, injury, abandonment or
  frightening transformation.
- Rumi's arrival reads as gratitude and friendship, not threat or jump scare.

## Gate 9 — delivery and provenance

- Every Grok request is 15 seconds or less; planned requests are 6s or 8s.
- Native download preserved unchanged with stable attempt filename.
- Starting frame and every reference alias recorded in
  `04_CONTINUITY_LEDGER.csv`.
- Identity, hands, topology, geography and camera review fields completed.
- Audio remains off during generation. Authorized voices/music are added later.
- Assemble at 16:9 for 1280×720 without cropping essential action.
- Rejected attempts remain labeled with their failure reason and are never
  reused as reference authority.
