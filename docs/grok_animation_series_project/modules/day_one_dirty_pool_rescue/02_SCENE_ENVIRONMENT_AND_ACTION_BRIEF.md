# Scene, environment and action brief

## Story purpose

- Episode/phase: Day One in the Mermaid Castle
- Sequence: Mermaid Pool rescue and first meeting with Rumi
- Emotional start → end: concern and neglect → care, relief, wonder and trust
- Child-readable action: Roshan sees four obvious problems, helps each one in a
  fixed order, saves the seahorse last, and earns a new friend's gratitude
- Authored assembled duration: approximately 60 seconds
- Grok generation limit: each shot 15 seconds or less; planned shots are 6–8s

## Fixed environment geography

The room is a symmetrical lavender shell-and-pearl chamber composed for 16:9.
Never mirror it.

- A huge oval aqua pool fills the lower two-thirds of frame.
- A broad pearl rim curves across the foreground.
- Left wall: large cream shell lounge with short aqua steps.
- Left-of-center rear: scallop-shell rainbow waterfall, around 29% of frame
  width from the left.
- Rear center-right: tall open arch showing calm blue reef depth.
- Right-of-center rear: magical lavender/aqua seahorse fountain on a
  pearl-and-coral pedestal, around 72% of frame width from the left.
- Far right: shell-shaped shelf with neatly folded pastel towels.
- Flower float rests left of pool center; star float rests right of center.
- Warm shell sconces sit at the extreme side walls.
- Roshan's arrival/readable home position is lower-left without covering the
  waterfall, seahorse or central pool.
- Rumi's reveal path begins below center and rises to the open center of the
  pool. Her final upright position stays near center and does not cover the
  seahorse's face or rainbow waterfall.

## Initial dirty state

The room is dim and neglected, not scary or toxic.

- Use a restrained dull olive/grey atmospheric wash over the normally bright
  aqua/lavender/cream palette. Preserve readable faces and landmarks.
- Pool surface: one broad, calm cluster of olive algae and harmless floating
  trash. Keep two or three visual groups, not hundreds of equal details.
- Rainbow waterfall: hanging seaweed ribbons and mineral grime interrupt the
  flow. The rainbow is faint/blocked, not redesigned.
- Foreground/right pool rim: a separate low grime-and-soggy-trash cluster.
- Seahorse fountain: exact established identity, visually tired and clogged.
  One soggy crumpled pink wrapper is visibly wedged sideways into the
  mouth/nozzle with a short soft olive strand tangled around it. The blockage
  must read as trash, never a tongue, wound, food, cigarette or body part.
- No Rumi, silhouette, braid or hidden face is visible before the rescue.

## Cleanup order and state continuity

1. `pool_surface` — Roshan removes the broad algae/trash cluster.
2. `rainbow_fountain` — Roshan clears hanging growth from the shell waterfall.
3. `pool_rim` — Roshan removes the separate rim grime/trash cluster.
4. `seahorse` — last and most emotionally important. Roshan gently extracts
   the pink wrapper and frees the nozzle while preserving normal hand ownership.

After each action, the removed material stays removed. Do not repopulate algae,
move debris to another landmark, or jump backward to a dirtier state. Light and
water clarity improve subtly after each cleanup, then complete in `SH070`.

## Finale transformation

- The seahorse brightens and resumes a clean, graceful water stream.
- The rainbow shell waterfall progresses from blocked/faint to its full ordered
  rainbow flow; it does not become a generic white waterfall.
- The olive/grey wash clears into luminous aqua, lavender, pearl and warm peach.
- Use a restrained pool ripple, sparse pearl glints and a few prismatic points.
  Do not bury the characters under effects.
- Rumi rises only after this restoration has visibly begun.

## Roshan acting

Roshan remains the approved young mermaid child. Her concern is readable
through eyes, brows and a gentle forward lean, not fear or crying. She helps
with calm determination and one clear physical action per shot. Her hands,
tail, tiara, hair and costume remain consistent. No legs, feet, shoes, tools,
weapons or new magic wand.

## Rumi acting

Rumi is safe, confident and warmly grateful. Her reveal motion vocabulary is:

1. broad slow swim upward from below center;
2. enormous braid and wide sleeves trailing with restrained lag;
3. body rotates/settles into an upright hover;
4. one broad friendly wave;
5. relaxed open-handed idle beside Roshan.

She is the exact owner-approved violet-braided character. Do not shorten the
braid, exchange jacket motifs, age her, darken her into an unrelated design, or
replace her turquoise/lavender/coral tail gradient with a solid violet tail.
