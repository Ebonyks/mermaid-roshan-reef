# Dirty Stuffie Room — perspective transition shot design

## Purpose

Use the ten character-free dirty-room perspectives as optional visual bridges
between primary story beats. They establish lateral distance, ceiling height,
foreground/midground/background layering and the entrance-to-nook axis without
adding more Dust Bunnies or delaying the rescue.

Authority folder:
`../../locations/stuffie_room/perspectives_dirty/`

## Recommended sequence use

- Storyboard A may use `P01`, `P07` and `P03` as short establishing/eyeline
  bridges. Do not insert all ten into the rescue; the emotional discovery must
  remain quick.
- Storyboard B may use `P04`, `P05`, `P06` and `P10` to make the basket sources
  and room width readable. `P09` is useful once per episode as a reverse
  orientation shot.
- `P02` and `P08` are optional breathing shots for a longer edit.
- Never show a bunny, Roshan or Baby Eagle in a perspective anchor itself.
  Character action is generated in a dependent complete full-frame shot.

## Motion and delivery locks

- The perspective still is a starting/reference authority, not a flat layer to
  translate, zoom, tilt or perspective-warp into final animation.
- Every changed action or camera frame must be a newly accepted complete image.
  No tween, morph, optical flow, cross-dissolve, cutout compositing or static
  background pan is allowed in delivered frames.
- Keep the full dirty state through these transitions. No web, dust, footprint,
  scrap or toy disorder vanishes before all four basket bunnies exit `SH050`.
- Audio remains off during generation. Add approved room tone and family voices
  only during editing.
- Keep each requested Grok shot at six or eight seconds and trim to the authored
  two-to-four-second beat after acceptance.

## Review gates

- Same room identity, architecture, prop designs and dirty-state palette.
- The room contains exactly three lights and two baskets; a viewpoint may
  naturally occlude or crop a distant landmark but never add or duplicate one.
  Exactly zero Dust Bunnies are visible.
- Strong near/middle/far depth separation without fisheye distortion.
- No mirrored landmarks, invented exit, extra basket or geometry teleport.
- No camera move may hide an identity or geography failure.
- Human approval is required before a perspective becomes continuity authority.
