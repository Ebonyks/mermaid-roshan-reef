# Storyboard A — Roshan discovers Baby Eagle

## Sequence definition

- ID: `MR_E01_SQ030_SC010`
- Title: `Dirty Stuffie Room Discovery`
- Authored assembled duration: approximately 20 seconds
- Shot count: five short shots
- Emotional start → end: curious entrance → shocked attention → gentle concern
- Ending handoff: Roshan understands that Baby Eagle needs help

## Continuity locks

- Roshan enters and stays on screen-left, looking inward/right.
- Exactly three Dust Bunnies exist throughout: one center-light swinger and two
  wing bunnies. No basket, floor or background bunnies.
- Baby Eagle uses the complete bag-free pinned pose. No backpack or body crop.
- Dirt dressing and subdued light remain unchanged across all five shots.
- Camera cuts are authored and restrained; room geography never mirrors.

## Panel direction

### Panel 1 — doorway wide

The wide composition establishes the exact room before Roshan becomes large in
frame. Keep the two foreground baskets at both corners and all three ceiling
lights visible. Roshan enters only a short distance from the left and stops
without crossing center. Baby Eagle may be a small low shape but is not yet
visually emphasized.

### Panel 2 — emotional punctuation

Cut in instead of pushing the camera continuously. Use one held shocked pose,
one blink and a tiny hand lift. The expression must be immediately readable to
a non-reader, but warm enough that the room never feels dangerous.

### Panel 3 — comic ceiling insert

The custom Dust Bunny swings from the center light, silhouetted cleanly against
the lavender ceiling. Show one full gentle arc with delayed ear/curl settling.
No second light bunny and no background bunny silhouettes.

### Panel 4 — redirected attention

Return to Roshan on screen-left. Her eyes and chin move down before the cut.
This short bridge prevents the Baby Eagle reveal from feeling spatially random.

### Panel 5 — sad reveal

Use the correct low bag-free Baby Eagle pose. One ordinary small Dust Bunny
plays on each spread wing with alternating little bounces. The bunnies do not
pin the head, body or neck. Baby Eagle gives a sad blink and small wing strain;
Roshan remains clear at far left. Hold the final concerned tableau long enough
for a four-year-old to identify the rescue target.

## Acceptance summary

The storyboard passes only if a silent viewer can answer: Roshan came from the
left; she first noticed a funny bunny on the center light; then she saw sad Baby
Eagle; and exactly two more bunnies were playing on the wings.
