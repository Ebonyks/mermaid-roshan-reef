# Storyboard B — dirty-room basket ambush and clean reveal

## Sequence definition

- ID: `MR_E01_SQ040_SC010`
- Title: `Dirty Stuffie Room Basket Ambush and Clean Reveal`
- Authored assembled duration: approximately 28 seconds
- Shot count: six short shots
- Emotional start → end: peaceful relief → comic surprise → brave playful win
- Starting handoff: the first three bunnies are gone and Baby Eagle is standing,
  but the room remains visibly dirty
- Ending handoff: final gust reveals the clean room, Baby Eagle confident,
  Roshan delighted

## Continuity locks

- Same unmirrored room, using the dirty authority through the beginning of the
  wing-blast shot.
- Baby Eagle starts standing at lower center-right. Roshan stays screen-left.
- The prior three Dust Bunnies are absent.
- Exactly four new Dust Bunnies emerge, all from the two foreground baskets:
  two from the left and two from the right.
- All four share the approved lavender Playroom Dust Bunny identity. The motion
  references are poses, not separate Boss/Rainbow/Grey/Mini character designs.
- Final distribution before the gust: one bunny on left light, one on center
  light, one on left floor lane and one on right floor lane.
- All four leave in Baby Eagle's single safe gust. None returns in the resolve.
- The room remains dirty until the fourth bunny has fully exited. Only the
  trailing gust clears the room into the clean authority.

## Panel direction

### Panel 1 — earned calm

Begin in the visibly dirty room with enough stillness to make the new movement
legible. Baby Eagle's
standing silhouette and both full baskets must be unmistakable. A small breath,
blink and feather settle are sufficient.

### Panel 2 — two-source warning

The baskets communicate the next event without text. The left basket compresses
and rocks once; the right answers. Stuffies inside bob but remain in their own
baskets. Do not reveal a bunny early.

### Panel 3 — exact four-way burst

The left basket releases a light swinger toward the left light and a twirling
floor bunny toward the left open lane. The right basket releases a light
swinger toward the center light and a bounding floor bunny toward the right
open lane. Stagger launches by a few frames for countability, but complete the
four arcs within one shot. The right light stays empty.

### Panel 4 — Baby Eagle chooses to help

Cut closer. Baby Eagle looks left light → center light → floor pair, then plants
both glitter feet and opens the wings backward. Keep the four bunnies in the
background or frame edges as stable spatial reminders. This is confidence, not
anger.

### Panel 5 — one decisive gust

Both wings sweep forward together once. Broad pastel wind ribbons connect the
action to all four bunnies. Light bunnies release their grips; floor bunnies
lift. The four travel in coherent arcs up and toward screen-right, spinning as
soft fluff with surprised but unharmed faces. No bunny pops, dissolves, strikes
a wall or becomes dust particles. Once the fourth bunny is fully outside the
frame, the trailing gust lifts the remaining webs, dust piles, lint, scraps and
floor marks and settles the room into the clean-state authority. Every changed
frame is a complete new generation; never use a wipe, crossfade, composite,
morph or static plate swap.

### Panel 6 — proud, quiet finish

Reveal the first clean wide of this storyboard. Baskets and toys are intact, all
lights still hang correctly and no bunny remains. Baby Eagle's feathers settle;
Roshan makes one small delighted bounce or clap. End on a stable friendship
tableau suitable for later approved chirp/celebration audio.

## Acceptance summary

The storyboard passes only if a silent viewer can count two baskets, see two
bunnies come from each, track two to lights and two to floor, understand that
Baby Eagle's wings create the gust, confirm all four leave safely before the
room changes, and understand that the trailing gust causes the dirty-to-clean
reveal.
