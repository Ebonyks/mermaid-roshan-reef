# Stuffie Room — approved geography

State authorities:

- `STUFFIE_ROOM_DIRTY_GEOGRAPHY.png` — every shot before the end of Baby
  Eagle's final gust;
- `STUFFIE_ROOM_CLEAN_GEOGRAPHY.png` — the final post-gust state only.

The 16:9 room is a broad lavender castle playroom. Never mirror it.

- Three circular shell-and-pearl ceiling lights: left, center and right.
- Large shell-shaped stuffie nook centered against the rear wall.
- Two large aqua toy baskets in the extreme foreground corners.
- Tent and round rug at rear-left.
- Stacking rings at lower-left of center.
- Cubes and tunnel at lower-right of center.
- Low cubby shelves flank the central stuffie nook.
- The broad open floor between the foreground baskets is the main acting lane.

The dirty authority is deliberately, materially neglected while remaining
warm and child-safe: dim grey-lavender light, readable webs around the upper
walls and lights, a dusty floor film with smudges and footprints, soft lint and
dust piles, harmless paper/fabric scraps, displaced toys, overfilled baskets
and a rumpled central stuffie nook. These are environmental story evidence,
not additional enemies or individual mandatory cleanup targets.

The dirty authority persists through Storyboard A and the basket ambush in
Storyboard B. After all four basket bunnies have fully left the frame, Baby
Eagle's trailing wing gust clears the remaining webs, dust, scraps and disorder.
Only then may the clean authority control the image. Never show the clean room
before that final causal reveal, and never alternate between the two states.

Do not add a bed, throne, pool, exterior view, extra toybox or fourth ceiling
light. Keep both foreground baskets fully visible whenever their movement or
contents matter. Architecture and fixed props do not move between state
authorities; only dirt and small movable-toy disorder change.
