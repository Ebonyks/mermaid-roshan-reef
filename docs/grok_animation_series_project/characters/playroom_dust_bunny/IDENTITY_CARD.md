# Playroom Dust Bunny — approved identity

Primary identity: `PLAYROOM_DUST_BUNNY_HOP_IDENTITY.png`.

Motion/pose authorities:

- `MOTION_LIGHT_SWING.png` — custom upside-down grip and light swinging;
- `MOTION_BOUNDER.png` — forward floor hop;
- `MOTION_TWIRLER.png` — sideways floor twirl.

These are small playful lavender Dust Bunnies, distinct from the larger Boss
Dust Bunny. Preserve a rounded cloud-of-curls body, two long spiral ears, small
pearl joints/paws, large glossy plum eyes, coral blush and a tiny delighted
mouth. They behave like weightless mischievous fluff.

They may bounce on Baby Eagle's wings, swing from a light, spring from baskets
and tumble in a gentle gust. They never bite, scratch, strike, carry weapons,
show teeth aggressively, become dirty realistic rodents or suffer injury. A
wing gust blows them harmlessly out of the room as soft spinning fluff; it does
not kill, crush, explode or painfully impact them.
