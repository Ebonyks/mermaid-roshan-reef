# Ember King — identity and continuity card

- Status: **APPROVED_PRIVATE_CANON** identity; motion package V1 is **REVIEW**.
- Stable ID: `ember_king`
- Role: child-appropriate antagonist, father of the Ember Prince, sole owner of
  the candle obsession that starts the journey.
- Relative scale: family scale anchor at 100%; the Ember Prince's standing
  silhouette is exactly four-fifths (80%) of his height.

## Immutable traits

- Adult volcanic turtle-dragon with a lighter stocky-athletic body, red/coral
  scales, cream muzzle and belly, and two small rounded fangs.
- Large asymmetrical black emo fringe over one eye; obsidian horn crown and
  natural shell with warm ember seams.
- Charcoal sleeveless vest, dark cuffs, chain belt, round ember clasp, and huge
  aubergine split cape tailored around the shell. No tie.
- Broad, readable silhouette and high-key plum/navy storybook contours.
- The fancy birthday candle motivates him but is not part of his reusable
  character atlas.

## Acting and motion

He is an adult ruler with the emotional presentation of a melodramatic teenage
emo kid: bored slouch, half-lidded looks, defensive embarrassment, possessive
enthusiasm and sudden delight he tries to hide. Feet plant first; torso and
shell compress next; cape arrives one or two beats late. He blunders, recovers
his dignity and never becomes a predatory monster.

## Attachment rule

Attach identity plus the accepted starting frame for normal shots. Add the
motion authority only for heavy walking, a cape fan or a blunder. Attach the
Prince identity separately for a family shot; never infer the son from the
King's design.
