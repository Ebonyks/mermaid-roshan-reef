## Immutable visual traits

- Young-adult mermaid with a reassuring, confident face; almond eyes, strong brows and pointed ears.
- Enormous sculptural violet-purple braided high ponytail with lavender value shifts and a tied loose tip.
- Star-shell earrings.
- Cropped lavender-and-navy sea-jacket with rounded shoulders, gold edging, original shell/coral-comet/wave motifs and wide sleeves.
- Modest pearly white high-neck shell top and gold three-shell clasp.
- One long tail: turquoise/deep aqua at the waist, through lavender/purple, into a broad coral-pink split fin; two small coral-pink side fins.
- Exactly two arms, two visible hands, one continuous mer-tail and one split tail fin. No legs, feet, shoes, wings or weapons.

## Acting and motion

Rumi reads as safe, affectionate, capable and playful. Prefer open hands, relaxed shoulders, gentle hover, broad readable wave and slow confident swimming. Her heavy braid and loose sleeves trail motion with restrained secondary action. The owner sample establishes that close hugging and cheek-to-cheek affection with Roshan are natural, welcomed interactions.
