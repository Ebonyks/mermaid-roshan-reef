# Baby Eagle — approved private-project identity

Primary identity: `BABY_EAGLE_STANDING_IDENTITY.png`.

Pinned-state authority: `BABY_EAGLE_PINNED_STATE.png`.

Baby Eagle is a young, plush-like rainbow eagle friend. Preserve the turquoise
and mint scalloped feathers, sunny-yellow face patches, pink crest and wings,
black wing tips, pale pink-and-black beak, large blue eyes and silver-glitter
feet. The standing image controls the complete body and normal proportions. The
pinned image controls only the sad low pose with wings spread on the floor.

Do not use `assets/book/baby_eagle.png`: that protected book image contains a
packed backpack and a lower-body crop, and it is the explicitly rejected
identity for this sequence. Never add the backpack, luggage, straps or a crop
below Baby Eagle's body.

Motion vocabulary for these storyboards: small breathing idle, worried blink,
careful stand, wing-flap anticipation, one broad safe double-wing flap and
feather settling. Baby Eagle is sad and inconvenienced, never injured.
