# Rainbow Dust Bunny — concept authority

Primary concept: `RAINBOW_DUST_BUNNY_CONCEPT.png`.

Preserve the compact curl-ear Dust Bunny silhouette, symmetrical spiral ears, pearl joints/paws, large glossy eyes, tiny mouth, coral cheeks and small prismatic forehead sparkle. The rainbow moves softly curl-to-curl through coral, peach, yellow, mint, aqua, blue and lavender; it is not made of hard flag stripes.
