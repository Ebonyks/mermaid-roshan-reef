# Paste this at the beginning of the Grok Project

```text
PROJECT: Mermaid Roshan — Animation Series

MISSION
Develop at least one hour of coherent 2D Mermaid Roshan animation across many short sequences. Each Grok generation is at most 15 seconds, but all outputs belong to one expanding series. Optimize for continuity and reusable world-building rather than novelty.

AUTHORITY IS DOMAIN-SPECIFIC
STYLE authority controls linework, paint treatment, lighting, motion density and camera language.
CHARACTER authority controls identity, age, anatomy, face, hair, wardrobe, accessories, color placement and scale.
LOCATION authority controls geography, architecture, recurring props and screen-side relationships.
SHOT authority controls pose, action, camera, screen direction and timing.
The accepted previous ending frame controls immediate temporal continuity when the next shot continues the same action.
If references conflict, do not blend them. Preserve each authority only in its domain and ask for a human decision if the conflict cannot be resolved.

VISUAL MEDIUM
Polished 1990s Japanese shōjo magical-girl television-animation language interpreted through Mermaid Roshan's established art: photographed-painted-cel clarity, expressive navy/violet contours, deliberate two- or three-tone paint bands, restrained pearl highlights, aqua/lavender/rose shadows, pastel watercolor/gouache backgrounds, elegant held key poses, controlled secondary motion and sparse prismatic effects. The era and production language control rendering, staging and timing; canonical references control identity. Do not copy another franchise's characters, costumes, symbols or exact shots. No photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy modern mobile-game splash art or liquid AI morphing.

BASE-VIDEO STYLE AUTHORITY
The owner-approved base video and its selected stills are the strongest authority for rendering style, shot texture, motion cadence, facial acting density, lighting behavior and camera restraint. They do not override canonical character designs or location geography. When the base video is not attached to a generation, attach the smallest relevant set of extracted style stills.

SERIES CONTINUITY
Every named character has one canonical identity card and a status: APPROVED, APPROVED_PRIVATE_CANON, CONCEPT, or REFERENCE_PENDING. APPROVED_PRIVATE_CANON is fully authoritative for this private family animation project. Never invent or silently redesign a REFERENCE_PENDING character. Never promote a CONCEPT design to canon without human approval. New characters are added through the onboarding template and receive stable filenames and version numbers.

CHARACTER SAFETY
Mermaid children remain children. Preserve species and mer-tail anatomy. No unintended legs, feet or shoes; no duplicated limbs; no fused bodies; no costume or color drift; no age change; no extra characters. Preserve hand and prop ownership explicitly.

PRODUCTION MODEL
Create and approve anchor stills before motion. Generate individual shots, never more than 15 seconds. For continuing action, use the accepted previous ending frame as the starting image. For an authored cut, make a new approved still using canonical character, style and location authorities. Preserve native downloads, stable names and attempt records. Assemble accepted shots externally into episodes and longer programs.

AUDIO
Visual generations default to audio off. Approved family recordings and licensed audio are added separately. Do not clone or synthesize family voices.

PROJECT HYGIENE
Use stable asset names. Mark every output APPROVED, REVIEW or REJECT with a reason. Rejected images remain historical evidence but are never reused as references. Search the Project before creating a new design. Do not let parallel agents independently redesign the same character, location or dependent shot chain.
```
