## 2. Initial observations, made before reading the prior critic notes

1. **The clean reveal shows a different fixture, not the same fixture cleaned.** The sink and toilet change geometry at completion. During the 0.75 s cross-fade both versions are visible at once (ghost basins).
2. **Scrubbing the tub produces no change in the tub.** The tub is the longest task, yet the only reward is three 2–3 px gleams.
3. **A preschooler's natural scrubbing does not finish the round fixtures.** Back-and-forth rubbing caps the sink and toilet at 57% indefinitely, and short tub strokes cap at 79%.
4. **The turn into and out of each leftward leg pops.** The whole tail flips to the other side of the body in a single frame.
5. **In the leftward carries the brush trails backward into the tail.**
6. **More than half the run is watching rather than playing,** and taps during travel are ignored.

## 4. Strengths to preserve

- **Propulsion is causal.** `thrustPlan()` gives zero travel with the tail disabled (`propulsion[].disabled = 0` in the receipts). This satisfies `ODR-ROSHAN-TAIL-PROPULSION-RESTART-20261101`; keep the motor.
- **The grasp order is correct.** The hand reaches the parked grip before `held` flips (`update()` grasp branch, `pickupError` 0), and the brush rides the posed palm socket through carry and work.
- **The interaction contract is solid:** passive and stationary input earn nothing, release keeps progress, the next touch resamples direction, and a live wardrobe swap keeps the clock, actor and contact.
- **The tub near-lip occluder works.** It is clipped below the pelvis (`paintFront()`, `lowerTail`), so the lower tail sits inside the bowl while the torso and working hand stay in front (frames 510–552).
- **The work-pose reach solve is well balanced.** The body moves only 17 px for 109 px of bristle travel in the tub, 19 px for 83 px at the sink and 4 px for 45 px at the toilet ([`trace_summary.json`](evidence/trace_summary.json)). The arm and brush absorb the stroke.

## 7. Deliverable 3: cold-start reconstruction checklist (raw art → replayable Godot)

Each step lists its input, output and check. Coordinates are logical 1024×576 (production applies ×1.25 to 1280×720; `DL-INT-04` requires one transform for touch, sockets and art).

1. **Verify the sources.** Input: packet manifest. Output: hash receipt. Check: `verify_packet.py` digest `ec6638ec…`.
2. **Register the plates.** Input: `dirty.png`, `clean.png` (or the C-01 registered variants), `fixture_outlines.json`. Output: one `Sprite2D` room plate plus three feathered `Polygon2D` clean cutouts, using production's `_add_fixture_cutout()` inner-ring feather (0.94/0.91) instead of a hard clip. Check: the C-01 IoU and offset gate.
3. **Import the rig.** Input: `roshan_arm_rebound.rigby` (`project.json` and textures). Output: one `Skeleton2D` with 17 `Bone2D` and skinned `Polygon2D` slots (`anatomy`, `bodice`, `shoulder_skin`, `hair_skin`, `tailwear`, `fluke_join`, `headwear`), plus `Ballet` and `Magician` skins as data-only attachments. The contract v2 adapter maps bone IDs to `roshan.canvas.shared`. Check: per-bone rest transforms match the source within 1e-4; visible-triangle strain 0.8–1.2 with zero flips at rest and over the `Roshan rig demonstration` animation.
4. **Bind the views.** Input: right-facing quarter view, authored left torso and head UV art (`left_*_uv.png`), and the front seed for T2. Output: a view resource per facing; the left head uses the 25×23 grid weights from `makeHeadGrid()` (head, ponytail_base, ponytail_tip, chest). Check: head size matches between views within 1%.
5. **Bind sockets and props.** Input: brush art anchors `GRIP` (670,320), `END_GRIP` (784,173), `BRISTLES` (305,850), scale 0.069. Output: palm sockets `screen_right_hand + (6,0)` and `screen_left_hand + (9,0)` (the left-hand grip bridge); the prop parented to the palm socket once held. Check: palm-to-grip error < 1 px and bristle-to-contact error < 1 px every frame (the packet reports about 1e-13).
6. **Build the state machine.** Phases `wait → approach → grasp → carry → ready ⇄ work → settle → (carry | done)`. Contract: the grasp flips `held` only at age ≥ 0.82 with the palm on the grip; release from `work` returns to `ready` keeping `progress`; the next touch resets the direction sample (`gesture.last/direction/pivot`); a wardrobe swap mid-phase keeps the clock, actor and contact; focus loss calls `release()`. Check: the reversal and swapview harness modes, ported as Godot probes.
7. **Load profile data.** Input: `acting_profile.json` (rebound02) and the per-fixture table (`center`, `r`, `need`, hover offset, lift height, gesture kind, voice cue). Output: one scene-profile resource. Check: the profile hash is recorded in the build receipt.
8. **Port the locomotion motor.** `STROKE` keys, `GLIDE_BEATS`, `thrustPlan()` (240 samples, drag 0.60 → +9.4 late) and arc-length route sampling. Check: tail-disabled ablation travels 0; firstThrust and peakSpeed match `propulsion[]` in the receipts within 2%.
9. **Port the action poses.** `relaxedArm()` (anatomical bend signs), `poseScrubArm()` and `scrubPose()` (reach solve, depth and foreshortening clamps), lift heights 21/23/28. Check: the work-excursion ratios in `trace_summary.json` (root 16–23% of bristle travel).
10. **Port layering and occluders.** Draw order room → brush → actor → front occluders; the tub `tubLip` occluder clipped below `root.y + 40`. Check: frame-step frames 510–552 for equivalence.
11. **Add feedback.** Bubbles every 12 px of credited travel, guides, completion cues (after C-08 and C-10). Check: the event log contains one completion per fixture.
12. **Verify in this order:** (a) stationary hold, off-target drag and passive input earn 0; (b) the gesture matrix; (c) release/resume at both tub ends; (d) live swaps in all 3 looks; (e) strain 0.8–1.2 over a full run in all 3 looks; (f) the trace metrics (turn displacement, brush lead, idle time); (g) 30 fps capture and frame-step review; (h) device, child and owner (separate evidence).
13. **Preserve for the next reviewer:** the build and profile hashes, the trace JSON, the gesture-matrix output, the turn and reveal frame-step sheets, and the failed variants with their reason (as `iteration_summary.json` does).
