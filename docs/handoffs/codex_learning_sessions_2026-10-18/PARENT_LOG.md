# Two-week check sheet for the grown-up

Part of the [learning-sessions work order](README.md). Section 3 is the
printable page; Codex typesets it with the question card (package QC1).

## 1. Why

The learning-value review closes with this test: "For two weeks, a parent
can note three things after each session: what she said or did off-screen
that used it, whether she chose it again, and any frustration. With one
child, watching beats scoring." This sheet is that test for the three
pieces in this work order: the book question card, Feelings with Puff, and
the quiet medals.

It is not a test of her. The Head Start framework the review used says its
goals are a lens and must never be used to decide that a child has failed.

## 2. How to use it

- After a reading or a play session, write one line. It takes half a
  minute. Skip days whenever you like.
- Do not quiz her to fill the sheet. Just notice what she says and does.
- Keep it at home. If you want the owner, Claude or Codex to adjust
  something, describe what happened in words; no photos or recordings of her
  are needed.

## 3. The sheet (printable)

**Two weeks with the book card, Grand Puff and the quiet medals**

Three things to notice after each session:

1. **Off-screen:** what did she say or do away from the screen or book that
   used it?
2. **Again:** did she choose it again by herself?
3. **Frustration:** none, a little, or a lot? What happened?

**Signs to look for**

- **Book card:** she answers more as the book gets familiar; she uses book
  words such as gunk, scrub, scoop, gently, tidy or "one little job at a
  time"; she retells three things that happened, in order; she asks for the
  book.
- **Feelings with Puff:** she names a feeling without being asked ("I'm sad
  like Puff"); she answers "How do you feel today?" with a feeling word; she
  says how someone else might feel.
- **Quiet medals:** she still picks her favourite games; she does not ask
  for medals or for "gold"; she uses praise words about herself or others
  ("I kept trying!"); she is not let down when a game ends without a medal.

## 4. After two weeks

- **Keep it** if she used it off-screen at least once, chose it again by
  herself, and showed no repeated frustration.
- **Change something** if the same frustration shows up twice, or she starts
  avoiding something she used to choose. Tell the owner which piece it was
  and what happened; each work package names what can be adjusted (fewer
  card questions, a shorter Puff beat, or a small finish moment brought
  back by a new owner decision).
- These notes are private family observations. They help decide what to
  keep; they do not close any audit finding.
