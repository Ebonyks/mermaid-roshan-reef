## Pool room composition

The room background is the strongest asset. Its shell architecture, coral,
turquoise water, rainbow waterfall position, arched view, and warm lavender
palette form a polished, coherent storybook room with a clear pool basin. The
composition remains stable through all states, and the cleaned rainbow is a
strong before/after landmark.

The activity cutouts do not consistently inherit that finish. Roshan, the two
dust bunnies, loose pool objects, seahorse fixture, basket, and interaction
props read as separately sourced stickers. Several have bright or dark matte
fringes visible at phone-scale size, especially the seahorse/plant silhouette,
dirty waterfall edge, net, and loose objects. Objects floating in the pool
rarely receive water contact shadows, submerged edges, ripples, or a shared
waterline treatment, so their layer ownership remains weaker than the painted
room beneath them.

The large caption card is readable but occupies most of the lower 110 pixels,
covering Roshan's tail, the lower-left bunny, and part of the pool rim. Text in
the card does not by itself prove the objective is clear to a non-reader. The
large Back control is clear and safely separated from the action.

## Skimmer

The six pieces of trash, large net, lower-right basket, and spread across the
pool make the collection goal readable. In
`01_real_room_skimmer_partial_held.png`, the net visibly overlaps a green
piece while input is still held, which is useful static evidence of causal
contact. The cleared state removes the loose trash without disturbing the
room.

The weak assets are the trash family and net. The trash resembles unrelated
decorative leaves, a flower, a star, a fabric scrap, and a sponge more than one
authored debris set. Scale, outline weight, lighting, and material vary. The
large net is crisp but has a hard sticker edge and little water contact; its
handle and basket do not share the room's softer painted contour. The basket
sits on the lower-right rim without a visible deposit transition in these
stills. Capture of the full flight/deposit path is still required.

Repair priority: unify the six debris pieces as a deliberate pool-cleanup
family, clean their alpha fringes, and add restrained shared water contact.
Retain the large net silhouette and generous contact radius. Reaudit pickup,
flight, basket landing, and settled removal as motion rather than stills.

## Waterfall

The cleaned rainbow waterfall is attractive, immediately recognizable, and
well placed in the room. Three vertical lanes and down arrows communicate the
required gesture. The partial held frame proves the left lane changes while a
real drag remains active.

The clogged overlay is the weakest activity asset. It reads as a tall green
curtain or seaweed panel pasted over the rainbow rather than contamination
attached to flowing water. Its rectangular silhouette, dense vertical texture,
hard perimeter and dark value mass break the room's rounded pastel language.
In the partial state, the scrubber is small and displaced toward the lower-left
edge while the broad lane changes above it; contact is less convincing than
the skimmer contact. The bright cyan/white reveal seam around the panel further
emphasizes the composite boundary.

Repair priority: replace or repaint the grime as three waterfall-bound organic
clog layers that preserve the rainbow fixture silhouette, use clean alpha,
and visibly part under the scrubber. Enlarge or reposition the scrubber at the
actual cleared edge and capture its held contact in every lane. Preserve the
successful clean rainbow artwork.

## Seahorse rescue

The seahorse remains a strong, child-readable focal character on the right,
and the eight yellow progress dots make monotonic progress visible. The
midpoint and last-tug frames show distinct progress counts. Bubbles help sell
activity around the obstruction.

The red-green obstruction resembles a floating flower or bundle suspended to
the left of the seahorse's snout. Its attachment point is unclear, and its
scale and dark contour dominate the face. It does not convincingly read as
trash being pulled from the mouth. The lower-right basket remains detached
from the tug path. The purple swimmer and land bunny compete with the rescue
subject and use a flatter outlined style than the painted seahorse and room.

Repair priority: clarify a small mouth contact point, keep the obstruction's
outward motion aligned to that point, and show an unambiguous final deposit or
removal. Reduce incidental competition during the rescue and apply consistent
water contact/edge treatment. Preserve the seahorse identity and broad
one-finger target.

## Finale and handoff

`07_real_room_rainbow_reveal.png` shows a valuable cleaned-room payoff: the
rainbow is active, the seahorse emits clean water, the obstruction is gone,
and a star burst appears. However, Rumi is semi-transparent while crossing the
center of the pool and overlaps the star, swimmer, and water pattern. This is
a busy transitional frame, not a settled identity or clean Rumi acceptance
frame. The mixed transparency makes Rumi read as a ghosted overlay.

`08_real_room_rumi_settle.png` does not visibly show Rumi settling. The actual
visible state is the next-room handoff. An oversized Playroom preview covers
most of the pool, obscures the center and right-side finale subjects, runs past
the right viewport edge, and is partially covered by the caption. The hand
pointer is separated above the preview border. This is a concrete HUD/layout
defect and prevents the image from serving as Rumi-settle or finale evidence.
The file should be described by its visible handoff state in any manifest;
its filename must not grant Rumi evidence.

Repair priority: hold the clean pool and fully opaque, unobscured Rumi long
enough for a separate settled capture. Then size the handoff card inside the
safe canvas above the caption, keep the complete preview and border visible,
place the pointer on the actionable region, and avoid covering the principal
room payoff. Reaudit finale active, Rumi arrival, Rumi wave/settle, handoff
appearance, handoff idle, and handoff touch as separate states.

## Disposition

The real-room capture corrects the earlier host-fixture evidence problem and
proves that the three activities can progress through real GUI callbacks in
the current Mobile-rendered composition. It also exposes material art defects
below 4.5: inconsistent sticker edges and water contact, an incoherent clogged
waterfall overlay, ambiguous seahorse-mouth contact, and a blocking oversized
handoff that covers the finale. The room background and clean rainbow should
be retained. Game-wide art acceptance remains open.
