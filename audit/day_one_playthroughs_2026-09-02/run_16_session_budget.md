### 5. Fit: two, three or four sessions?
- Four rooms + boss are 150 / 215 / 380 s of actual content. With navigation and a 1.3× dawdle factor the whole of Day One is 4.5 / 7 / 15 min. **Two sittings fit comfortably** (rooms 1–4, then the boss); **three** is the right plan only because (a) each Continue costs 25–45 s of unguided re-entry and (b) the boss must not be interrupted (S3-2), so it deserves a sitting that starts fresh; **four are never needed**.
- Trim/split/re-order to fit a 4-year-old: (1) start every Continue at the castle door or the current room, not the plane; (2) speak and point at the next door after Rumi and after the customizer (today both are silent); (3) shorten the two boss holds (9.6 s and 8.5 s) and give the first window the assist that today only arrives after two certain misses; (4) let the boss re-arm after an interruption; (5) the playground and hall bunnies are good agency filler — keep them, but never let them be the only glowing things.
