# Run 02 — Tap-everything toddler
Persona: She is four, delighted, and treats the phone as a drum. She taps every ~0.3 s wherever her eye lands — Roshan, sparkly things, the words at the bottom, the big round buttons, the bunnies, the glowing door, and anything that reacted once she taps two or three more times. She does not drag on purpose; when something moves under her finger she may hold and scribble for a moment. She wants noise, motion and sparkles, and she wants them now.
Path taken: New Game from the start menu (first-ever run, no confirm dialog) → Sky Lagoon at the ocean gate, where the very first pointer and voice line invite her to tap the pearl plane; she does, and is dumped into the 3D Reef (recoverable via the Rainbow Portal, two taps) → back in the lagoon she taps the right edge, Roshan walks, the doorstep auto-enters Pearl Castle → main hall: she pops the three hall bunnies, meets Daddy's bubble, spams the foggy doors (swish/voice each tap), eventually taps the one golden door → Bubble Bath: one tap on the basket, then a long stall at the sink because taps never scrub (only a held ≥2 s scribble does), tub tap-to-drain works instantly, another stall at the tub brush, then she taps the pool picture → Mermaid Pool: skimmer, waterfall and seahorse are all tap-driven and she blasts through them → elevator picture-map to the Playroom: two taps pop the two pinning bunnies, Baby Eagle flies, the stuffie picker opens, she mashes until TAKE ALONG → Craft Room: seven tap targets, desk, attack customizer, confirm star → silence: no voice tells her where the boss door is; she wanders via elevator/back to the hall, walks right, taps the Royal Hall door, floor-taps cancel the walk twice, then it lands → Grand Puff: splash + showing (taps only sparkle), then her continuous tapping lands three-tap rounds almost by accident → Day Two transition and banner → she is dropped into free swim (out of scope).

## Time budget
- Total: **168 s / ≈380 s / ≈1320 s** (2.8 min / 6.4 min / 22 min). Typical assumes one short reef excursion is avoided; with a 90 s reef detour typical is ≈8 min. The two gesture stalls (beats 9, 11) dominate variance — this persona has no natural reason to hold a finger down for 2 s.
- Longest passive span: **beat 25, 9.6 s** (boss splash 3.24 s + showing 6.4 s, every tap answered only with a sparkle). Runner-up: beat 27, ≈8 s (win beat + 4.18 s transition).
- Longest input-required span with no new feedback: **beat 9, up to 60–180 s** — the sink accepts presses but produces no visible progress for taps; grime alpha is gated by `min(arc, distance, time/2.0)` so the clock reset per press keeps it at zero (`day_one_bathroom_cleaning.gd:834-846, 299`). - Natural stopping points that lose nothing: after the basket tap (`_queue_save` 1.5 s, `main.gd:7196-7205`), after the bathroom completes (`_write_save` `:7247`), after each pool activity commit (`_queue_save` `:7294-7302`), after each pin pop (`_write_save`, `castle_rooms_25d.gd:4124`), after TAKE ALONG (`companion.gd:688`), after each art action (`_queue_save` `:7256-7262`) and the customizer confirm (`_write_save` `:7268-7270`), and the boss win (`_write_save` `:7082`). ## Pros (as this player experienced it)
- Everything that is a tap target is big: basket 220², pool picture 205×190, elevator 136², back 112², seahorse = whole screen, bunny cards, confirm hearts 330×150 (`StorybookUI.MIN_TOUCH` 110, `storybook_ui.gd:23`).
- Nothing she taps is ever "wrong": blocked doors flutter and speak, the boss's shielded taps poof and giggle, the showing/struck/friends beats answer with a sparkle (`dust_boss.gd:693-701`), and the customizer dim quietly eats stray taps (`attack_customizer.gd:257-261`).
- The pool is built for her: press-to-catch skimmer (`pool_skimmer_activity.gd:229-233`), tap-assist waterfall (`:217-224`), tap-anywhere seahorse (`:207-210`). She clears it in under a minute and feels like a genius.
- The playroom pins pop on tap, not only by walking (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:2129-2132`), so the rescue is two taps.
- The boss reads her cadence: landing radius ≤4 keeps every open-window tap in reach (`dust_boss.gd:664-670`), the mercy ramp grows the window after 2 and 5 misses, and taps outside the window cost only a giggle.
- Every tap-driven state advance is idempotent/guarded (`_busy`, `_hunt_completed`, `_drain_reaction_count`, `mini(taps+1, 8)`, `_completion_started`, `_emit_once`), so rapid double/triple taps never double-fire progress, voice or save.
- Voice spam is rate-limited per (speaker,event) key by `min_gap` and by "same stream already playing" (`audio_director.gd:13-33`); `show_msg` adds a 0.5 s gap (`:169-172`).

## Cons / friction (as this player experienced it)
- The first thing the game points at and asks her to tap (the pearl plane) is the one tap that leaves Day One (`sky_lagoon_promenade.gd:264-269,333-336,801-803`). For a tapper this is close to guaranteed.
- Both bathroom gestures are tap-proof by design, and the per-press reset of `_valid_motion_seconds` (`day_one_bathroom_cleaning.gd:299`) means her natural "tap-tap-scribble-tap" never accumulates the 2 s. She sees the demo hand circling but no grime fades, so there is no partial-reward gradient for what she is doing.
- Inside the castle there is no caption at all (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:967-968`); every `show_msg` there is voice-only, and several speakers ("Pearl Castle", "Daddy Mermaid", "Baby Eagle", "Rumi") may fall back to the pitched "yay" if their clips are missing (`audio_director.gd:22-49`) — I cannot verify which clips exist.
- After the customizer confirm the game goes silent; the "All four rooms are clean! The big back door is glowing!" line exists but only on the placeholder path she never takes (`main.gd:7176-7187`).
- Blocked-door feedback has no sfx cooldown (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:1711`), so 0.3 s taps produce a swish every tap; the Royal Hall alone has a 2.8 s cooldown (`:1701-1709`).
- The pause corner is claimed before any GUI on every touch press (`touch_ui.gd:917-923`), so top-right taps open the pause sheet in every beat, including under the stuffie picker and the customizer whose dims visually cover the gear.
- The elevator toggle and its close button share the same 136² rect (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:1236-1242,1621-1627`): a double tap opens and closes the map, a triple tap reopens it (harmless, but confusing flicker).
