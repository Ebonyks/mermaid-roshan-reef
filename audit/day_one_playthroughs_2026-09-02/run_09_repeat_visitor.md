## Pros (as this player experienced it)
- Going back is *allowed*: completed rooms stay OPEN in the door language and the elevator (`castle_door_language.gd:51-52`), so her favourite loop never hits a locked door once a room is done.
- Clean rooms are not dead pictures: each of the four rooms keeps four authored tappable fixtures with atlas animations and sounds (bath: tub bubbles, faucet, flushing toilet, squeaking duck; pool: waterfall surge, seahorse spray, flower, star; playroom: waving stuffie nook, stacking rings, blocks, tent flap; craft: idea board, paint table, palette, ribbon) — `castle_rooms_25d.gd:480-546`, `776-823`. For a repeat visitor this is real play value.
- The clean bathtub refills and the dust-bunny swimmer comes back for a splash (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:3656-3658`, `1928-1949`) — an accidental but delightful "he's back!" moment for this persona.
- Completed-room hooks never re-fire the completion logic: `complete_activity` refuses completed rooms (`director:283-291`), `apply_day_one_cleanup` is idempotent (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:1872-1883`), so no revisit can re-dirty or re-complete anything.
- The boss rematch is explicitly friendly and idempotent (`director:436-447`; win text "Grand Puff is your friend", `dust_boss.gd:612-619`) and the reef portal re-arms after 3 s (`main.gd:9032-9033`).
- Hall dust bunnies stay popped within a castle visit (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:2664-2669`, `4064-4069`) but respawn on the next castle open (`922`) — a repeatable pearl snack for her.
