## Pros (as this player experienced it)
- The tell is genuinely picture-first: gold strobe at ~3.5 Hz, additive halo, 👆 hand, and the action button swapping ◇ WAIT → ✋ BONK! all switch on the same instant (`dust_boss.gd:490, 923-925, 940-946`; `touch_ui.gd:96-99`).
- Tapping the big fluffy thing is the bonk — the world tap counts on his card regardless of distance (`main.gd:5245-5252`; `dust_boss.gd:656-672`), and he leaps to her, so a rooted child never has to aim or walk (`dust_boss.gd:453-458`).
- Nothing she does is ever punished: shielded taps poof, bumps shove without cost or lock (`dust_boss.gd:703-709, 837-858`), and a missed window only lengthens the fight (`531-543`).
- The gentle assist after two misses (window +4.0 s, prowl −1.6 s, tighter landing) is what actually rescues her, and it arrives fast — by the third window of every phase (`dust_boss.gd:92-100, 361-366, 368-377`).
- The three-tap ladder has a wordless 1-2-3 in sound and picture: rising pitch tap → boing → chime, with a flinch per tap (`dust_bunny_boss_sprite.gd:24-30, 202-216`).
- Bumps get the only recorded voice in the fight (`roshan_bump.ogg`), and the win gets `roshan_win.ogg` (`ls assets/audio/voices`; `main.gd:9034`).

## Cons / friction (as this player experienced it)
- Every one of the nine instruction lines in the fight ("too puffy", "when he JUMPS and his star FLASHES", "tap FAST — three times", "coming closer", "slowed down", "DIZZY", "CROSS", "POOF", "tap the glowing TWIRL") reaches her as text plus a pitched "yay": no `roshan_dustboss_*.ogg` exists and there is no `roshan.ogg` family fallback (`audio_director.gd:24-49`; `main.gd:1019-1020`). The stress test flagged this on 2026-08-02 (§8) and it is still open.
- Wrong-time taps are answered every 2.6 s with the same happy "yay" as the right ones (`dust_boss.gd:718-722` → `audio_director.gd:171-173, 46-49`). For a non-reader the voice channel says "good" whatever she does.
- The first two windows of every phase are unwinnable for her by construction: the window closes 0.95 s after the leap (0.81 s in the final round) and her first tap arrives at 1.5–3 s. Six guaranteed misses per fight, each ending with Grand Puff's angry puff (`dust_bunny_boss_sprite.gd:239-243`).
- The gentle assist is thrown away after every round: `preassist_tier()` derives from the streak, which `_on_round_done` zeroes (`dust_boss.gd:366, 1176-1177`). The file's own comment says a slower child "never has to prove the same limitation three times" (38-39) — that is true only of the tier-5 mercy she is unlikely to reach.
- Strong mercy (miss 5) needs three further misses of 4.75 s windows in a row; for a constant tapper that is ~5–10 % per phase, so the ramp the design leans on for "the slowest hand" mostly never fires for her, while the two nine-second holds (beats 18, 21) fire every time.
- The mastery card takes her stars away live: ★★★ → ★★☆ at bump 2 → ★☆☆ at bump 3, diamond greys at bump 1 (`dust_boss.gd:1017-1034`). With him landing within 2.8 units of her after every assisted window and every round, she is shoved as soon as he moves (`772-802`, landing radius `397-403`), so she watches herself lose two stars by mid-fight.
- The 💜 round pips never appear: `_update_hud` writes `hud_game`, which main hides every frame (`dust_boss.gd:948-952`; `main.gd:9741-9744`). Partial progress (1 or 2 taps landed) is not shown anywhere either; `db_taps_this_round` is stored but never rendered (`1207-1208`).
- The dodge button competes with the star: an incoming hop makes ⚡↻ pulse at 10 Hz with a bobbing ▼ (`dust_boss.gd:1038-1051`), and after every assisted miss he is adjacent so the pulse fires almost every prowl — a second glowing "tap me" at exactly the moment she is told to wait for the star.
- The ending is a pile-up: "Two down!" is overwritten in-frame by "CROSS now"; the victory line is overwritten in-frame by the Day Two line; the bronze medal card (2.2 s) lives entirely under the 0.25 s black and the 4.18 s transition; `roshan_win.ogg`, a "yay", the fanfare and the medal chime start on the same frame (`dust_boss.gd:1197-1202, 600-610`; `main.gd:7086-7106, 9034`; `medal_system.gd:30`).
