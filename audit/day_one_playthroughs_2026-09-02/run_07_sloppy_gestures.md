# Run 07 — Sloppy gestures, weak fine-motor
Persona: She is four, engaged, and wants to "clean the castle", but her hand does not do what she means. Her circles are small lumpy ovals (~90 px across, radius ~45), her strokes are short (~120 px) and slow (~150 px/s), she drifts 50–120 px off whatever she is aiming at, she lifts her finger part-way through a gesture, she freezes mid-stroke for 1–2 s, and she gets the verb wrong half the time (drags when a tap was wanted, taps when a drag was wanted). She cannot read; only the ghost hand, the pulsing star, motion, glow and sound reach her.

Path taken: New Game → Sky Lagoon (the only pointer aims her at the plane/Reef; assumed she is steered past that and walks/drags to the castle, auto-entering at the doorstep) → Main Hall → shell-elevator picture map → Bubble Bath: basket tap, sink circular scrub (the first motor wall), tub tap → "NO!" drain, tub back-and-forth brush (second motor wall), pool picture → Mermaid Pool: skimmer (easy), waterfall lanes (easy once she hits the strip), seahorse 8 taps (trivial) → Rumi → elevator → Playroom: tap/bump two pinning bunnies (easy) → elevator → Craft Room: 4 materials + 3 grime buttons (small release-mode buttons, many silent misses), desk, attack customizer confirm star (no pointer) → silence → elevator → Main Hall → walk right → royal-hall door → Grand Puff (drags do nothing; the action button works; mercy ramp carries her) → Day Two banner over the reef.

## Time budget
- Total: **190 s (3.2 min) / 336 s (5.6 min) / ≈750 s (12.5 min)** — the max caps the two bathroom gestures at 60 s each; for a child whose touches never reach 2.0 s of continuous motion the sink and tub are **unbounded** (see hazards).
- Longest passive span: boss splash + showing, 9.64 s with no accepted input (`boss_splash_2d.gd:19`, `dust_boss.gd:60`); the dizzy hold after round 1 is 9.6 s (`dust_boss.gd:573-574`). Both exceed the ~8 s "taps the screen / looks away" threshold; taps answer with a sparkle only.
- Longest input-required span with no new feedback: the sink and tub scrubs — each failed touch produces grime that visibly *re-dirties* on the next touch-down and no voice/banner after the first 5 s; typically 12–20 s, worst case open-ended. Second: the art studio, where each miss walks Roshan somewhere (a wrong answer) for up to ~30 s.
- Natural stopping points that lose nothing: after the basket tap (`_write_save` via record, debounced 1.5 s), after each bathroom step (`day_one_record_bathroom_cleanup_step` → `_queue_save`, `main.gd:7192-7197`), after each pool piece/lane/tap (`main.gd:7294-7302`), after each bunny pin (`_write_save`, `castle_rooms_25d.gd:4118-4122`), after each art item (`main.gd:7255-7261`), after the customizer (`_write_save`), after the boss (`main.gd:7081`). Only in-flight sink arc/distance and a partial lane are lost.

## Pros (as this player experienced it)
- Skimmer, seahorse, waterfall, tub-drain tap and playroom pins are all **press-based**: her "drag instead of tap" costs nothing there (`pool_skimmer_activity.gd:229-230`, `pool_seahorse_rescue_activity.gd:179-182`, `pool_waterfall_activity.gd:153-158, 217-224`, `day_one_bathroom_cleaning.gd:453-457`, `castle_rooms_25d.gd:2118-2121`).
- The seahorse accepts a tap anywhere on the screen (`pool_seahorse_rescue_activity.gd:207-210`); the skimmer clamps her finger into the pool and catches within 118 px (`:274-296`); the waterfall clears a lane after a handful of downward-moving frames and even a still tap adds 0.22 (`:204-213, 222-224, 346-355`).
- Sink/tub touch-down bands are enormous (314 px around the sink centre; ±382 px in x for the tub, any y) so drift never rejects a gesture start (`day_one_bathroom_cleaning.gd:288-292`). Arc, distance and reversals are banked across lifts (never reset after `setup`).
- Her lumpy ovals satisfy the tub recognizer (each loop ≈180 px of \|Δx\| and 2 reversals) and the sink recognizer counts `abs(Δangle)` so an off-centre oval still accumulates arc.
- Playroom: tapping the bunny card pops it outright, and merely walking through the 205×155 px contact ellipse also pops it; blocked doors always answer with a swish, banner and voice (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:1695-1718`).
- Boss: the assist ramp is real and persistent — after two misses the window is 4.75 s, after five it is 10.25 s with a free tap, wider reach and slower hops; bumps never cost progress (`dust_boss.gd:361-403, 495-519, 557-561`).
- Saves are frequent and additive; a stop anywhere loses at most the current gesture.

## Cons / friction (as this player experienced it)
- **Sink and tub demand ≥2.0 s of moving frames inside a single touch, and that clock is zeroed on every touch-down** (`day_one_bathroom_cleaning.gd:299`, gate at `:494-496` and `:511-513`, accrual `:356-357`). Lifting mid-gesture — her defining habit — restarts the clock every time. Pauses do not reset it but do not count either.
- The grime overlay alpha is `0.72 × (1 − min(arc, dist, time))` (`:838-844, 851-856`); because the time ratio is 0 on a fresh touch, the sink/tub *visibly re-dirties* every time she puts her finger back down. The only "progress bar" (`_progress`) is declared but never created (`:110, 819-831`). Her failed attempts therefore get feedback that reads as **"you undid it"**, not silence.
- For the tub, motion time only accrues on frames where accumulated \|Δx\| > 2 px (`:501-503`); slow, slanted or oval strokes qualify on only ~60 % of frames, stretching the 2 s gate to 3–4 s of real motion per touch.
- `SINK_MAX_GESTURE_SECONDS`/`TUB_MAX_GESTURE_SECONDS` = 5.0 are never enforced (`:26, 28`, only reported at `:192, 194`): a 6 s stroke is fine; a 1.5 s stroke does nothing and its time is discarded on lift.
- No instruction is ever spoken as words: every objective uses `_say("roshan","talk")` → the generic 1.05 s `roshan_talk.ogg` (`audio_director.gd:13-27, 169-173`; measured clip lengths: talk 1.05 s, win 0.84 s, fail 1.24 s, bump 1.21 s, intro4 1.04 s, wacky_fail 2.85 s, sparkle 1.12 s). Banners are text. Her guidance is the demo motion only, and it is not repeated after the 5 s banner.
- Every Button in the flow (basket 220², pool picture 205×190, elevator 136², elevator cards 180×138, art targets 92–144 × 72–104 art-px, customizer confirm 116²) is Godot default release-mode: a 120 px drag that exits the rect fires nothing and says nothing.
- Art studio misses fall through to the room root and walk Roshan (`day_one_art_studio.gd:64`, `castle_rooms_25d.gd:2115-2154`) — a confusing "wrong answer"; the smallest required targets (paint bottles 115×130 stage px, grime 165×95) are at the edge of her 50–120 px accuracy.
- The attack customizer has no pointer and no voice for its only required control (the ✦ star), and eats stray taps silently (`attack_customizer.gd:257-261`).
- After the art room completes there is no banner, no voice and no on-screen pointer toward the royal hall (`main.gd:7273-7283`; the celebratory line at `:7180-7184` is unreachable for art). The door cue is on an off-screen hall door.
- In the boss fight her drags are classified as `WORLD_MOVE` and never become a tap (`touch_ui.gd:739-747`, `main.gd:5179-5181`); a held tap that wobbles >22 px (`TAP_SLOP`, `touch_ui.gd:93`) is dropped too. Only the action button fires on press.
- Day One's opening pointer aims at the plane, whose first tap flies her to the Reef (`sky_lagoon_promenade.gd:333-336, 801-804`); `first_session` is false before the promenade builds (`main.gd:3975`), so the intended first-session gating never applies.
