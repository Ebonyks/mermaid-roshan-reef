# Run 15 — Little artist
Persona: She is four, she has her own paint smock, and today she opened the game because she wants to *paint*. She taps whatever looks like a brush, a bottle or a colour, she is willing to tidy up if it gets her to the paints, and she will happily stay in any screen that lets her pick colours. She cannot read; she follows the finger, the sparkle and Roshan's voice. She has ~10 minutes.
Path taken: New Game → Sky Lagoon arrival → castle main hall → bathroom → pool → stuffie playroom (all cleared as fast as the code allows, one line each below) → elevator to the glowing Craft Room card → Day One art studio: taps the four loose supplies (the first and third taps hijack her into the Castle Logo Studio for a while — she enjoys it), then the three grime smears, then the glowing desk → attack customizer (tries every colour and both effects, hunts for the way out, finds the gold ✦) → the room finishes in silence → she taps the back arrow, lands at the far left of the main hall, and needs an adult to know that the glowing Royal Hall door is off to the right → Grand Puff → Day Two.

## Pros (as this player experienced it)
- The studio really is picture-first: each action is one big tap (smallest hit box 115×130 px, all ≥ her 50–120 px aim), the 👇 always sits on exactly one next target, and every tap answers with sparkles, a station animation and a Roshan voice (`day_one_art_studio.gd:282-305`, `352-374`, `418-438`).
- Order-free within each phase: any supply, then any smear; a tap in the 45 px overlap between brushes/pink or blue/cups still counts (`refresh_from_state` 85-119). Nothing can fail; a miss just walks Roshan to the tap (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:2115-2154`).
- The desk unlock is a real "look at that" moment: gold star + three pulsing rings + the only "win" voice in the room (`156-163`, `387-388`).
- The accidental Castle Logo Studio is, for *this* child, the best part: six colours, eight symbols, big buttons, her logo then hangs in the castle rooms (`castle_logo_studio.gd:262-330`, `466`). She would call this "painting".
- The customizer's choices are big (104×104 / 150×110), pre-selected so a random ✦ still works, and the effect previews retint live so colour taps feel like painting the bubbles (`attack_customizer.gd:263-269`).
- Nothing is ever lost: additive save keys, restore re-derives desk/customization from the item maps (`day_one_director.gd:526-533`, `585-628`).

## Cons / friction (as this player experienced it)
- "Clean seven things then pick a colour" is a tidy-up chore with a colour swatch at the end; she never gets to *make a mark*. The only mark-making in the room is the palette/paint-table idle animations she cannot steer. The customizer is titled and framed as an **attack** profile she has no context for — she has never attacked anything (Day One bunnies are bumped/popped), and the card shows a cleaning brush, dots and bubble/splash frames with no word-free explanation of what they are for (`attack_customizer.gd:3-9`, `172-181`, `185`).
- The choice never pays off where she will look for it: `dust_boss.gd`, `dust_bunny_boss_sprite.gd`, `boss_splash_2d.gd` and `octagon_stage.gd` contain zero references to `attack_color`/`attack_effect`/`HitEngine` (grep count 0/0/0/1-comment); the only consumer is `combat_arena.gd:478-500` (ice/pepper/clean dungeon shots + `HitEngine.show_attack_feedback_2d`), which is behind the jobs lock during Day One. So the pink splashes she picked are invisible in the Grand Puff fight that immediately follows.
- Every instruction in the room is the same clip: `_speak_cue` always calls `_say("roshan","talk")` (`day_one_art_studio.gd:391-393`), which is "This is so much fun!" (`tools/make_voices.py:47`). The banner text that carries the actual instruction is hidden inside the castle (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:967-968`), so even a parent beside her cannot read "Now scrub the left counter grime!". The 👇 is the *only* instruction — it works, but it is a single point of failure.
- The first tap of the room (brushes) and the third (blue paint) throw her into a different full-screen activity 1.44 s later, twice, with a different exit button each time (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:3672-3680`). Delightful for her, but it hides the studio, resets her attention, and the Day One rule says "The castle jobs are resting today" (`main.gd:7141-7145`).
- The customizer is a soft trap: the dim swallows every tap (`attack_customizer.gd:257-261`), the full-rect root is `MOUSE_FILTER_STOP` on layer 18 above the castle's 14 and its controls (`main.gd:6999-7003`), so the back arrow (28,28) and ↕ (1116,544) are unreachable; there is no voice, no pointer and no back-key path (`main.gd:1164-1178`); the only exit is an unlabeled gold ✦ at (870,110).
- The climax is silent: completing the fourth room produces no voice, no fanfare, no banner (and the banner would be invisible anyway), no pointer; she is left in an empty craft room with the elevator arrow bouncing. From there the glowing Royal Hall door is ~2,665 hall-art px (~2,040 screen px) to the right of where the back arrow drops her, and the elevator does not list it.
- The "win" cue at desk unlock double-talks (`roshan_win` + `roshan_talk` simultaneously).
