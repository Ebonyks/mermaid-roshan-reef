# Run 06 — Quit-and-resume
Persona: Same 4-year-old, same phone, but today real life keeps interrupting: the home button, a dead battery, a sibling grabbing the phone. The app is killed at the worst moment of every beat of Day One (19 kills), and each time she — or a grown-up — reopens the app a little later and taps ▶ CONTINUE. She expects to be exactly where she was. She cannot read; she follows voice, pointers and whatever glows.

Path taken: New Game → killed during the Sky Lagoon plane arrival → Continue → Sky Lagoon again (plane replays) → castle main hall → killed before the bathroom → Continue → Sky Lagoon → castle → bathroom; killed mid basket tap, mid sink circle, after the sink, mid tub brush, during the sparkle finale, and on the pool picture — each time Continue drops her back in Sky Lagoon and she re-walks into the castle and through the golden door → pool: killed at skimmer 3/6, waterfall 1/3, seahorse 5/8 and during the Rumi rise (the rise replays in full) → playroom: killed with one pin cleared (the cleared pin stays gone), then killed inside the stuffie picker (the room is now "sparkly clean" and the picker never comes back until Day Two) → art room: killed at 5/7 and with the desk customizer open (both resume cleanly) → Royal Hall → Grand Puff: killed after one hit → **Continue lands in Sky Lagoon with `giant_dust_bunny_boss_triggered` already on disk; the Royal Hall is now BLOCKED, no door glows, and Day One cannot be finished from the castle.** The last two kills (during the WIN beat, during the Day Two transition) are traced hypothetically, because a real child never reaches them after kill #17 unless she stumbles into the reef's Dusty Attic portal.

The mechanics every row below leans on: `_write_save()` is synchronous (`scripts/main.gd:3949-3959`); `_queue_save()` arms a 1.5 s debounce (`main.gd:3983-3988`) that the `_process` tick actually writes 1.5 s after the *last* queue (`main.gd:9727-9734`); going to the background flushes a pending/dirty save (`main.gd:4336-4357`, `NOTIFICATION_APPLICATION_PAUSED`/`WM_CLOSE_REQUEST`) but writes nothing when nothing is queued; the commit is transactional (`scripts/save_state.gd:694-718`) so a kill mid-write cannot corrupt. Continue always calls `_launch_from_start_menu()` → `_enter_level2_now(false,false,true)` (`main.gd:3966-3972`), i.e. **Sky Lagoon at the ocean gate, never the castle or a room** — no room id is ever saved (`save_state.gd:34-46`, director `serialize_state` `scripts/day_one_director.gd:462-489`), and `day_one_current_room` is re-derived from the completed list (`day_one_director.gd:539-551`). Every Continue also wipes `m.g` (`main.gd:5771`).

## Time budget
- Total: with 19 interruptions the run never reaches Day Two through the castle. To the dead-end at K17: **min ≈ 14 min / typical ≈ 22 min / max ≈ 35 min** = a clean Day One (≈ 10–14 min, inferred from the other runs, not proven here) + 17 × re-entry tax (min 15 s / typical 30 s / max 60 s each: R0–R4 above) + redo work (sink 2–5 s, tub 2–5 s, pool partials 2–10 s, Rumi replay 3 s, K1 plane 7 s, boss restart ≥ 60 s). After K17 the remaining time is unbounded unless an adult knows about the reef portal.
- Longest passive span: R0 app relaunch + world build + Sky Lagoon build — 4–10 s of nothing she caused (inferred; `main.gd:986-1075` builds the whole world before the menu). In-game: the K1 plane arrival (7.0 s, `sky_lagoon_promenade.gd:33`) and the boss WIN floor (up to 38 − active seconds, `dust_boss.gd:68,595`).
- Longest input-required span with no new feedback: the Sky Lagoon walk to the castle on every Continue — 6–9 s of edge taps with only the walk animation; the castle glow sits at alpha 0.055 until focused (`sky_lagoon_promenade.gd:786-789`).
- Natural stopping points that lose nothing (home-button kill): after the plane departs (7 s); after the basket tap, the sink, the drain, the tub (each once the 1.5 s debounce or the pause flush has written); the pool picture; after every pool catch/lane/tap; after each pin; the art taps; the ✦ confirm; the boss door glow; Day Two. **Not** safe: inside the stuffie picker (adoption lost), any moment of the boss fight and its WIN beat (Day One lost), and mid-gesture partials (redo only).

## Pros (as this player experienced it)
- The bathroom, pool and art rooms restore to the exact step: tools already in the basket, the brush parked on the rim, three trash pieces gone, five seahorse tugs banked, four supplies put away — and each resume re-fires the room's own voice line and pointer (`day_one_bathroom_cleanup.gd:137-143`, `day_one_bathroom_cleaning.gd:807-816`, `day_one_pool_cleanup.gd:378-392`, `day_one_art_studio.gd:376-389`).
- The Rumi reveal replays in full after a kill mid-rise (`day_one_pool_cleanup.gd:75-78`), so the reward beat is never half-seen.
- Rescue pins are written synchronously per pin (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:4118-4122`) and rebuilt from `stuffie_wins` (`:4153-4167`); a cleared bunny never comes back.
- The home button flushes the debounce (`main.gd:4355-4357`), so on a normal Android backgrounding nothing queued is lost; the transactional writer (`save_state.gd:694-718`) means a kill during a write can't corrupt.
- `normalise_save_patch` heals partial saves in her favour: tools authorized at supply 2, tub drained at step 2, desk unlocked at 7/7, completed rooms clipped to the fixed order (`day_one_director.gd:539-629`).

## Cons / friction (as this player experienced it)
- Every Continue restarts in Sky Lagoon, not in the room she was in (`main.gd:3966-3972`); the ~30 s walk-and-hall tax is paid 17 times, and the golden door is off-screen every single time (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:2323` vs door feet `:172-190`, view `:86-87`).
- The first thing she hears on every Continue is "Tap the pearl plane to visit the Reef!" with a pulsing ring on the plane (`sky_lagoon_promenade.gd:266-269,1077-1083`); the plane is a single-tap exit to the 3D reef (`:333-336,801-808` → `main.gd:8440`) with no way back for a fresh save (`main.gd:5632-5637`, `9669-9675`).
- A kill after the first boss hit ends Day One for good in the castle (K17); the WIN beat can hold her for up to ~30 s in that same unsaved state (K18).
- A kill inside the stuffie picker silently deletes the adoption for the rest of Day One (K14): the room says "sparkly clean" and Baby Eagle is gone.
- The lagoon flips day↔night on almost every relaunch (`save_state.gd:93-94`), so "why is it dark now?" happens over and over.
- Bathroom re-entry stacks roshan_win and roshan_talk 0.38 s apart (different `said_cool` keys, `audio_director.gd:14-19`) and, after a finale kill, snaps to the clean room with no crossfade or sparkles (K7).
- A hard kill (battery) within 1.5 s of any tap loses that tap; rapid seahorse taps keep resetting the timer so a whole burst can be lost (`main.gd:3983-3988,9731-9734`).
