# Run 05 — Door explorer
Persona: She is four and in an exploring mood: every arch, picture button, plane and toy is a door to try, and the glowing one is the last thing she will obey. She wants to see what the foggy doors do, what the elevator does, what is back outside, and she repeats anything that made a noise. She plays the whole of Day One this way, with the back button as her favourite key.
Path taken: New Game → Sky Lagoon spawn beside the pearl plane, which the game itself tells her to tap → (branch) one tap flies her out of the onboarding into the open Reef; she comes back through the lagoon gate and is told to tap the plane again → slide and swing (two taps each) → walks right to the castle, taps the door twice → main hall, spawned on the LEFT half where all four visible doors are foggy → taps Dream House, Library, Kitchen, Opera (blocked), pops two hall dust bunnies (Daddy's bubble appears) → opens the elevator, taps five foggy cards, sees the one gold card, closes it → back button out to Sky Lagoon, rides the seesaw, re-enters → walks right twice, taps Playroom/Craft/Pool (blocked) and the Royal Hall (blocked, 2.8 s cooldown) → finally the golden Bubble Bath door → basket, sink circle, tub tap, tub strokes → pool picture (only exit) → pool: three skimmer catches, back out, revisits the clean bathroom (one-way again), pool picture, finishes skimmer/waterfall/seahorse, Rumi (no onward cue) → back, walk right, playroom: pops one pin, leaves, returns, pops the second, closes the stuffie picker → craft room: five actions, leaves, returns, two more, desk, customizer, confirm (no onward cue) → back, walk right twice, Royal Hall now gold → Grand Puff → Day Two banner, landing in the 3D reef at the castle anchor.

## Time budget
- Total: 237 s / 524 s / 1120 s (≈4.0 / 8.7 / 18.7 min). Without the reef-plane branch (beat 3) the typical run is ≈434 s (7.2 min). The typical run fits the 8–15 min window only because the reef detour is short; the max case (plane detour + repeated door hunts + a mercy-tier boss) blows past it.
- Longest passive span: beat 34 boss "showing" 6.4 s (dust_boss.gd:60) — restless but survivable; beat 35 Day Two overlay 4.18 s. In Sky Lagoon the 7 s plane bob is not passive (she can act) but ends with a one-frame vanish.
- Longest input-required span with no new feedback: the golden-door hunt (beats 7-16): every blocked tap returns the SAME swish + "yay" + fog flutter with no pointer toward the goal; for this persona that is 60–120 s of identical answers before she reaches the right half of the hall. Second: the sink circle (beat 19) 2–5 s with only grime alpha as feedback.
- Natural stopping points that lose nothing: after bathroom completion (`_write_save` main.gd:7249), after pool (7319), after each playroom pin (castle_rooms_25d.gd:4127) and the rescue (7333), after art (7282), before the boss (7794), after the boss (7081). Mid-sink/tub loses only the current stroke counters (steps persist; day_one_bathroom_cleaning.gd:157-165 restore). Mid-skimmer/waterfall/seahorse and mid-art lose at most the last action if the app dies inside the 1.5 s `_queue_save` debounce (main.gd:3983-3989, 7302, 7261).

## Pros (as this player experienced it)
- Nothing she tapped ever hurt her: blocked doors answer with a soft flutter and a swish, never a lock, a black screen or a lost tap (castle_rooms_25d.gd:1695-1718, 1640-1651 "keep the card tappable").
- The elevator is honestly the best map in the castle for her: one gold card among eleven foggy ones is readable without words, and blocked picks leave the menu open so she can keep comparing (1751-1762).
- The Royal Hall's 2.8 s cooldown proves repeated mist taps do not spam voice or SFX (1700-1709).
- Every room she abandoned mid-way remembered her: pool masks, art items, playroom pins and bathroom steps all live on main and are re-derived on entry; pins survive an app kill (4149-4167; save_state.gd:214-216, 274, 528-530).
- The playroom rescue works for both a "tap the creature" child and a "walk into it" child (2130-2134, 4022-4050).
- Sky Lagoon is a safe sandbox with no fail state: toys, animals and the castle door all light up on the first tap and act on the second (319-339).

## Cons / friction (as this player experienced it)
- The very first instruction of the game tells her to tap a "door" that leaves Day One (beat 2/3), and it repeats every time she comes back into the lagoon from the reef.
- On every hall entry she is spawned on the left half where all four doors are foggy and the golden door is off-screen; the only moving pointer (▼) points at the elevator (beat 7). She must "discover" the right half by tapping empty floor — twice.
- Blocked-door feedback is text plus a generic "yay" (Daddy has no clip) and never points at the golden door; the voice line she does hear (roshan_talk) is the same clip that also means "success" elsewhere (beat 8).
- After the bathroom, the back button and elevator disappear and stay gone on every revisit of the clean bathroom (beats 21-24) — for a back-button explorer that reads as "the game broke".
- Pool and art rooms end in silence: no "next door glows" voice, banner or pointer (beats 26, 32); the line that says so (main.gd:7182-7186) is unreachable on the real paths.
- The pause sheet's big secondary button reads "🌊 REEF" from anywhere in the castle or lagoon and drops her into the 3D ocean (pause_menu.gd:141-143, 261-262).
- Hall dust bunnies pay a pearl each and summon Daddy's bubble — a stronger reward loop than the objective, and they respawn after each courtyard hop (castle_rooms_25d.gd:1097, 4091-4096).
- After Grand Puff she lands in the 3D reef at the castle's hidden anchor, not back in the castle she came from (beat 35).
