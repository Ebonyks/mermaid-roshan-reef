# Run 04 — Impatient skipper
Persona: Four years old, has played tablet games before, and wants the NEXT thing. She taps through every banner, demo, reveal and celebration trying to skip it, taps the destination before the pointer arrives, and starts her next gesture before the previous demo has finished. Taps come ~0.3 s apart when she is spamming, ~1 s apart when she is engaged; she cannot read a single banner, so only voice, pointers, motion and sound reach her.

Path taken: New Game (no confirm on a fresh install) reloads the scene and drops her at the Sky Lagoon gatehouse beside the pearl plane, which is the only highlighted object on screen and carries the banner "Tap the pearl plane to visit the Reef!" — she very probably taps it and is flown out of Day One into the legacy 3D ocean (fork, ~60 %). Assuming she is brought back (or ignored the plane), she tap-walks right, probably double-taps a playground toy on the way (5.4 s locked ride), double-taps the castle door and lands in the Main Hall. The golden Bubble Bath door is two screens to the right; she taps one or two foggy doors ("…is resting"), then the bouncing ▼ elevator and the one gold card. In the bathroom she taps the basket (sponge flies 0.38 s), scrubs the sink ~3 s, waits 0.70 s for the brush, taps the tub ("NO!" 1.04 s), brushes ~3 s, waits 0.92 s for the sparkle, taps the pool picture. In the pool she skims 6 trash, scrubs 3 waterfall lanes, taps the seahorse 8 times, sits through the 1.73 s Rumi rise, then has to find the exit herself. Hall → playroom: she taps the two pinning bunnies directly (no walking needed), sits through the 0.72 s eagle flight, hits TAKE ALONG in the stuffie picker, finds the exit again. Hall → craft room: seven taps on the pointed items, taps the glowing desk, taps ✦ confirm — and gets silence: no banner, no voice, no pointer to the boss door. She wanders back to the hall, walks to the far-right Royal Hall door, and the Grand Puff fight begins: 3.24 s splash (taps eaten), 6.4 s showing (taps sparkle), three rounds of 3-tap windows separated by 9.6 s and 8.5 s holds, a 3.4 s befriending (5.3 s on a first-try run because of the 38 s floor), then the 4.18 s Day Two card (taps eaten). She ends in the free-roam 3D reef at the hidden castle anchor.

## Pros (as this player experienced it)
- Almost nothing before the boss is gated on her: banners never block input (audio_director.gd:169-173), the sink/tub "demos" are ambient guides she can scrub straight through (day_one_bathroom_cleaning.gd:370-393), and every handoff is under 1.8 s.
- Early input is never punished. Wrong lane, wrong order and spam taps all still progress (pool_waterfall_activity.gd:217-224; pool_seahorse_rescue_activity.gd:201-210; day_one_art_studio.gd:282-305); boss bounce-offs are a poof, not a penalty (dust_boss.gd:703-722).
- Double-tapping an ordinary hall door collapses the walk to 0.16 s (castle_rooms_25d.gd:4479-4503, 2270-2281): impatience is rewarded.
- Big targets everywhere she needs them: basket 220×220, pool picture 205×190, TAKE ALONG 330×150, seahorse "anywhere", bunny cards ~330 px, catch radius 118 px.
- Milestone saves at every room and 1.5 s debounced saves inside the pool and art room mean a mid-room stop costs at most one gesture.
- The boss preassist after two misses (window 0.75 → 4.75 s, dust_boss.gd:92-100, 368-377) means a masher lands every round by attempt three; mercy at five misses adds a free tap.
- The bathroom hides the back button, elevator and HUD so there is nothing wrong to tap (main.gd:7704-7725).

## Cons / friction (as this player experienced it)
- The first and only pointer on the arrival screen points at the exit: the plane is focused and announced, the castle is off-screen and its affordance is at 5 % alpha (sky_lagoon_promenade.gd:266-269, 1077-1083, 789).
- The fight she was racing toward is 3 rounds of ~0.75 s taps inside ~60 s of unskippable ceremony; taps during splash/showing/holds/friends/Day Two are eaten or answered with a sparkle — 36 s hard-forced plus ~25 s of prowl with nothing to do.
- POSITIVE_PACING_FLOOR = 38 s punishes exactly this player: a first-try run sits 5.3 s in "friends" with the boss already imploded (dust_boss.gd:68, 595-598).
- Not one boss or Day Two line has a recording (dustboss_show/tell/dizzy/hit/angry/win/again/closer/dodge, day_two_begins): she hears a pitched "yay" nine times while text she cannot read teaches the mechanic (audio_director.gd:22-27, 43-47; assets/audio/voices).
- Three bathroom handoffs drop a press that arrives early (0.38, 0.70, 1.04 s); her stroke then does nothing until she lifts and presses again (day_one_bathroom_cleaning.gd:449-450, 305-307). The 0.58/0.42 s pool holds swallow taps the same way.
- The sink instruction loses its voice to the 0.8 s min_gap right after the basket tap (day_one_bathroom_cleaning.gd:816; audio_director.gd:15-19).
- After the art confirm: silence. No banner, no voice, no pointer, and the Royal Hall is not on the picture map (main.gd:7264-7283, 7788-7793; castle_rooms_25d.gd:299-305).
- No room ever points at its exit; the only persistent pointer in the castle is the bouncing ▼ over the elevator, which also keeps bouncing during pool and art activities (castle_rooms_25d.gd:1258-1270).
- Banners overwrite each other in the same frame (dirty castle, "Let's clean together!", victory) so their paired voices are suppressed by min_gap; invisible to a non-reader anyway.
- A playground double-tap on the way to the castle costs 5.4–5.8 s with every tap eaten (sky_lagoon_promenade.gd:320-321, 160).
