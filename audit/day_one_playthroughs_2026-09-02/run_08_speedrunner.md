## Pros (as this player experienced it)
- The elevator picture-map is a legal 2-tap shortcut to every Day One room, so a child who knows it never has to do the two blind walk legs in the hall (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:1248-1271,1751-1762`).
- Every room's first target is live the instant the room shows (basket button, skimmer, pin cards, supply buttons) — no announcement gates input (`day_one_bathroom_cleanup.gd:120-143`, `day_one_pool_cleanup.gd:283-299`, `day_one_art_studio.gd:57-70`).
- Pool activities chain with only 0.58/0.42 s beats and the seahorse accepts taps anywhere on the screen at any cadence (`pool_seahorse_rescue_activity.gd:201-225`).
- The playroom rescue honours the game-wide "tap the creature" verb: two taps, no walking needed (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:2127-2133`).
- The picker's heart works at tutorial step 0, so the three-step colour lesson is skippable by someone who already knows (`companion.gd:818-826`).
- Grand Puff leaps to within 4 units of her and reach is 12, so timing is the only skill; hybrid taps on his card count from anywhere (`dust_boss.gd:72,397-403,656-671`).
- Every step is saved before the next; a phone-away at any room boundary costs nothing (`main.gd:7228,7249,7319,7332,7282`).

## Cons / friction (as this player experienced it)
- The 0.75 s three-tap window (0.65 s in round 3) is not reachable at the child model's 0.9–1.6 s reaction; even the balance probe's own "speedy" bot (0.55–0.70 s reaction, 0.18 s drum) lands its third tap ≈1.0 s after the flash. A real fast child therefore misses twice per round by design before the +4 s assist arrives, turning a 44.8 s floor into a 74 s typical fight (`dust_bunny_boss_sprite.gd:20-21`, `dust_boss.gd:92-97,361-366,495-503`, `probe_dust_boss_balance.gd:30-34,336-352`).
- 9.6 s and 8.5 s of forced hold after rounds 1 and 2 for a child who already knows what "dizzy" means; the showing (6.4 s) cannot be skipped and taps are answered with sparkles that look like "it's working" (`dust_boss.gd:418-434,573-574,693-701`).
- Starting a gesture during a `_busy` window in the bathroom (sponge travel 0.38 s, sponge-return+brush 0.70 s, drain 1.04 s) silently drops the press AND every drag after it until she lifts her finger — the fast child's first stroke is lost three times in one room (`day_one_bathroom_cleaning.gd:285-287,305-307,448-477`).
- After the art room there is no banner, voice or pointer toward the Royal Hall; the golden arch is in another room and off screen when she gets there (`main.gd:7273-7283,7792-7793`, `castle_rooms_25d.gd:188-190,2318-2324,4429-4431`).
- The Sky Lagoon arrival focuses the reef plane, whose activation exits Day One's hub; the castle door has a 10 %-alpha idle glow and needs two taps (`sky_lagoon_promenade.gd:266-269,695-696,801-808,1077-1083`).
- Inside the castle the HUD layer is hidden, so no caption ever shows and, with `roshan.ogg`, `rumi*`, `daddy.ogg`, `roshan_dustboss_*`, `roshan_day_two_begins` absent, Rumi's reveal, the blocked-door line, every boss teaching line and the Day Two banner are a pitched "yay" (`castle_rooms_25d.gd:967-968`, `main.gd:3590,3617`, `audio_director.gd:21-27,43-47`; file listing of `assets/audio/voices`).
- Two silent full-scene rebuilds in a row (boot, then New Game reload) before she can touch anything (`start_menu.gd:274-275`, `main.gd:986-1078`).
- She ends Day One standing in the retired 3D reef at the castle anchor, not in the castle she just cleaned (`main.gd:11066-11073,9042-9053,8817-8818`).
