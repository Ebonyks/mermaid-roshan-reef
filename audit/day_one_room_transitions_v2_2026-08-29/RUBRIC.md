# Day One four-room transition rubric

Established before production edits on 2026-08-29. Owner clarification on
2026-08-29 defines these as Luna/Sol evidence-based implementation scores;
intended-child observation and Lenovo Tab M11 acceptance are separate,
non-scored external gates.

Each room receives five scores from 1.0 to 5.0; the room score is their
arithmetic mean and the four-room cumulative score is the room-mean average.

1. **Dirty-state hierarchy** — dim/dark dirty room, unmistakable non-text
   target, and clear Roshan/target silhouettes at phone scale.
2. **One-finger non-reader agency** — large forgiving target, voice and
   pointer, one necessary active action, no fail state or derailing control.
3. **Ordered causal animation** — one saved beat per touch and an immediate
   visible environment/character response before the next target.
4. **Clean reveal and payoff** — strong contrast, animated and settled reveal,
   readable final hierarchy, and meaningful story payoff.
5. **Technical/provenance safety** — true 2D, exact Godot 4.7.2 Mobile,
   protected originals preserved, approved-art reuse first, texture/overdraw
   budget, compatible monotonic saves, and focused regression evidence.

Score anchors: 5.0 means no known deficit in inspected captures/probes; 4.5
means strong implementation with one remaining validation or hierarchy gap;
4.0 means a material clarity/response/payoff/technical deficit; 3.0 partial;
2.0 weak or ambiguous; 1.0 absent, broken, unsafe, or contradictory.

Requested target: every room at least 4.75 and cumulative mean at least 4.85.
