## Run bounded passes

1. **Discovery:** start at the ordinary visible invitation, allow its normal voice and pointer, and observe the first action without naming the gesture. Record adult help, idle hints, accidental actions and whether the pictured target explains the task. Stop when the player chooses to leave.
2. **Tactile control:** try an intentional press, slow drag, brief lift, wobbly retry and a miss; check visible acknowledgment, finger ownership, target separation and earned progress. Test rapid input, second-finger interference and cancellation in a separate technical run, preserving the child's play session.
3. **Timing:** record action-effective time, travel/contact time, teaching/narrative holds, the longest forced wait, number of repetitions, time to first success, recovery after a miss and completion. Compare first-time and repeat visits. Code constants are starting hypotheses, not observed durations or accepted floors.
4. **Replay and continuity:** let the player choose the next activity or replay. Interrupt a separate technical test mid-action, resume/reload and re-enter through the ordinary route; earned milestones persist and unfinished work cannot complete invisibly. Keep child response, owner review and machine verification separately recorded.

## Timing worksheet and repair decision

For each observed run, record `build`, `profile`, `route`, `activity`, `first_time_or_repeat`, `cue_start/end`, `input_start`, `visible_acknowledgment`, `arrival`, `contact/work`, `progress`, `completion`, `exit`, `misses`, `adult_help`, `voluntary_replay` and `player_chose_stop`. Keep timestamps and durations in seconds; record visible response in video frames and the recording rate. Record unmeasured fields as null. No numerical fun grade is needed.

Choose the smallest repair for an observed cause. A missed valid input needs routing/queue repair; an unclear action needs a visible/voice cue; an unreadable payoff needs stronger state change; a long purposeful journey needs an agency/timing review. Increasing particles or shortening every animation does not establish improvement. Reuse approved art and voices, preserve local contact and progress, run the focused negatives plus unchanged full gates, then repeat the same normal-route/device observation. The [repair stages](REPAIR_GUIDE.md) define closure requirements and current priorities.
