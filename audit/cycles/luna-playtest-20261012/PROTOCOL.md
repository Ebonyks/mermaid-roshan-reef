## Assignment and evidence

Read matrix.json and take only your assigned Lxx row. Read master audit planning entry and task index, relevant design AGE/INT/UI/MOT/SAVE/QA rules, related active findings and ledger entries. Do not rely on historical counts. Read the assigned gameplay source and probe; follow callers sufficiently to determine live entry, instructions, accepted input, progress, completion, replay and exit. A probe setup that writes state directly is INTERNAL_PROBE, not visible-route proof.

Execute `python -X utf8 -B audit/cycles/luna-playtest-20261012/review_tools/run_session.py Lxx` once from this worktree. The helper uses the exact approved Godot, isolated profile, unchanged existing probe and retained stdout/receipt. If it fails, retain the failure and classify it; do not patch runtime or tests. One bounded rerun only if needed to diagnose contamination/timing, use helper `--attempt 2`. Headless render is not Mobile visual evidence. Tool wall time is not child session length. A verdict containing ALL OK proves only its assertions.

Create ONLY `audit/cycles/luna-playtest-20261012/reports/Lxx.json` with schema `luna_playthrough_report/1`, session_id, reviewer (agent_id your canonical task name, model gpt-6-luna), baseline, focus, runtime_receipt (relative path), evidence_kind (INTERNAL_PROBE or ROUTED_INPUT_PROBE based on inspection), route_status (proven scope and route gaps), verdict (PASS/FAIL/INCOMPLETE), timing (exact code constants/source lines, estimates explicitly assumptions; never report game duration from tool wall time), tactile (target size, press response, drag/hold/release, ownership, cancellation), play_appeal (agent hypothesis about agency/variety/world response/replay; fun_score null), observations (array objects: title, classification CONFIRMED_RUNTIME/CONFIRMED_CODE/HYPOTHESIS/COVERAGE_GAP, evidence with exact file:line and log references, rules, existing_findings, reproduction, child_impact, proposed_repair, acceptance_test), strengths (with evidence), acceptance_gaps. Include 3-8 useful distinct observations; absence of defects may be a strength. Keep source line claims verified. Write your own findings, do not copy other reports. Cap initial review to the named focus and existing relevant code; comprehensive probes may exercise sibling games but you do not claim reviewed those.

No production/art/assets/config/workflow/git changes. Never read secrets, keystores or private identifying child data. Do not lower tests or fabricate evidence. Report blocked/missing voice recording approval separately. Passing probes cannot establish biological tactile feel, visual rendering, real device latency/frame pacing, child comprehension/fun or owner acceptance. These remain pending under DL-QA-01..07. Dormant/debug routes are evaluated as mechanics and entry gaps, not live content. New MA records and canonical edits belong to root after independent reproduction; this report supplies candidate intake.
