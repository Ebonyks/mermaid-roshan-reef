# DKC album production plan

Target a complete review album by November 12 and a refined print by November 19, 2026. The first week establishes coverage; the second is reserved for musical correction, full-song development, mixing and album translation. The 30 review rows currently imply 21 long-form pieces and 8 short entries. Local byte comparison reconciles K. Rool’s Cacophony and Game Over to the same underlying ROM sequence 15; keep both review rows and develop one musical arrangement while checking presentation/tail differences. Nothing is silently omitted because a source mapping is incomplete.

The current three reference performances are Simian V4, Island V6 and Aquatic V5r3. Mines, Funky and Fear Factory form the next production batch. Their new prints are production drafts for review, with the same no-synth direction and source-identity checks. All 27 music-driver entries now have direct game-audio references: six retained earlier captures and 21 new captures including Forest. The new captures match their ROM payloads and roundtrip losslessly to the captured PCM. Seven cues also have detailed hardware traces. The other cues still need per-note, loop, bend and instrument stress tests before claimed source fidelity.

## Completion criteria

Every row needs an exact source or a documented source-gap disposition, an acoustic call sheet, a recognizable first statement, purposeful full form or appropriately short signal, complete review audio, editable stems, and measured delivery evidence. Source maps, program names and successful renders do not establish musical acceptance. Record actual listening decisions and owner approval separately.

The next new musical batch after the six foundation pieces is Forest Frenzy, Treetop Rock and Bonus Room Blitz. It tests whether the established band can retain three different grooves without turning them into variations of Island Swing. Later atmospheric and boss groups deliberately test different ends of the palette. A true bright marimba and more wind articulations remain useful auditions; the installed Balafon 2 and existing recorded flute Hit provide honest working options meanwhile.

Keep the album private during development. The game recordings, extracted guides and installed commercial library sources stay local. Public reference links and production documents do not constitute permission to redistribute the source ROM, ripped samples or library material. No Mermaid Roshan runtime replacement or release is part of this album plan.

## Source reconciliation

The [SPC catalog](https://snesmusic.org/v2/profile.php?selected=711&set=) lists K. Rool’s Cacophony as a20-second entry. Local comparison of the previously archived SPC audio RAM with the owner’s ROM establishes an exact resident match to driver 15, named Game Over by the extractor. This resolves the underlying sequence, rather than proving every recorded intro or tail identical. The same comparison finds resident sequence 26 for Level Goal, Bonus Room Opened and Extra Life Balloon; for the latter two, resident background data does not identify the audible SFX trigger. Bonus Win contains sequence 18. Bonus Lose needs a version/payload comparison because no complete resident sequence matches this ROM exactly. The private reconciliation receipt retains all 29 SPC hashes and comparisons.
