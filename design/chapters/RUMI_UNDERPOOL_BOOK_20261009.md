## Story first

Roshan wants to join Rumi's evening song but thinks her voice is too small. She learns to follow a current beneath the bubble tram and helps Duri recover a courier bell. Duri promises to meet them after his stage delivery. The trio's rehearsal makes room for Roshan's little note. When the city's answering voices stop, Roshan asks everyone to listen. Duri is tapping beneath the bridge: the imps' scenery blocked his delivery, and the crates slid when he tried to move them. Roshan and Rumi assess the snagged banner before physically freeing him, with Hana and Nari supporting them. At the bathhouse, Duri admits how hard it was to be heard, and Roshan shares her own worry. A towel joke and shared couch make room for him; the friends hum softly and share snacks. The existing imps interrupt with their show announcement and banner. Duri identifies whose crates blocked his delivery, and Roshan leads the investigation. His courier knowledge then guides the group to the blocked current channel. Roshan asks the imps to make space; their refusal leads to a playful costumed fight. The trio keeps access clear while Roshan follows the current beneath their bubble puffs. She asks for help with the last crate. Physical teamwork opens the existing channel, and Duri carries the answering song around his delivery route. The imps tidy their props and join in. Roshan's voice belongs among everyone else's; the wider missing-candle plot stays open.

Rumi, Hana and Nari form the three-friend singing trio. Roshan remains the protagonist, joins their singing, notices the rescue, makes the decisive repair and returns to her familiar pool.
