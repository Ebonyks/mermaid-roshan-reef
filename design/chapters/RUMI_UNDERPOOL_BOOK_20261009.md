## Story first

Roshan wants to join Rumi's evening song but thinks her voice is too small. The city's answering voices have stopped. Between the trio's verses Roshan hears a turtle tapping beneath the bridge. Roshan and Rumi physically free him. Duri, an agent-named local turtle using the existing aquatic-turtle design, shows them the blocked current channel. Performer and magician imps have piled stage props against it to keep the concert to themselves. Listening, teamwork and a playful costumed fight open it again. The existing current carries everyone's song. Roshan learns that a little voice can make a big difference; the wider missing-candle plot stays open.

Rumi, Hana and Nari form the three-friend singing trio. Roshan remains the protagonist, joins their singing, notices the rescue, makes the decisive repair and returns to her familiar pool.

## Character candidates

- **Hana:** tallest of the trio; copper-red loose waves with a half-up twisted crown and one celadon shell clip, coral long-sleeved performance jacket over a cream high-neck blouse, celadon scales and deep-teal fan fin. Confident choreographer who learns to slow down and make space. No film ponytail, black graphic crop top, yellow mini-skirt, sword or copied markings.
- **Nari:** shorter and a little younger than Rumi/Hana; dark navy chin-length bob with curled ends and a silver crescent clip, mint hooded jacket, peach collar/scarf, lavender tail and pale peach fan fin. Funny, observant rhythm singer; first to follow Roshan's listening cue. No double buns, copied costume or weapons.
- **Roshan / Rumi:** inspected existing Pop Star Roshan and Rumi identity sources control face, colour, costume and one-tail anatomy. Narrow Roshan fabric ribbons must remain fabric, avoiding the known second-tail reading in `MA-ROSHAN-005`.
- **Duri:** existing lavender turtle with mint floral shell; a new supporting name and courier role, not a claim of earlier book canon.
- **Imps:** reuse existing purple glitter-jacket performer and navy-coated/top-hatted magician with trousers and shoes. Comical bubbles, soft props and bows; no injury or frightening weapons.

## Pacing and staging

34-page reading draft: cover, title, 30 story pages arranged as 15 illustrated spreads, refrain page and back cover. A quiet pool invitation opens; the city expands; the first song fails; listening narrows attention; physical rescue pays off; bathhouse gives recovery; clues connect to the obstruction; concert fight expands into three comic beats; repaired current makes the encore possible; pool homecoming settles the ending.

Recurring landmarks: pool rainbow/seahorse fountain, hanok lane, projecting shop pictograms, bubble tram/pearl bridge, mint tiled bathhouse, shell concert stage. One focal action per illustration. Comic panels occur for listening, rescue and the three action beats; the city reveal and encore stay broad and calm. Art contains no reading-dependent signage. Book prose is typeset separately.

Original refrain: "Little voice, big blue sea. / Sing with you, sing with me. / Make a space and let it through. / There's a place for me and you."
