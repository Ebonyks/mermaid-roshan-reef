## Story first

Roshan wants to join Rumi's evening song but thinks her voice is too small. She learns to follow a current beneath the bubble tram and helps Duri recover a courier bell. Duri promises to meet them after his stage delivery. The trio's rehearsal makes room for Roshan's little note. When the city's answering voices stop, Roshan asks everyone to listen. Duri is tapping beneath the bridge: the imps' scenery blocked his delivery, and the crates slid when he tried to move them. Roshan and Rumi assess the snagged banner before physically freeing him, with Hana and Nari supporting them. A bathhouse song restores his confidence. His courier knowledge then guides the group to the blocked current channel. Roshan asks the imps to make space; their refusal leads to a playful costumed fight. The trio keeps access clear while Roshan follows the current beneath their bubble puffs. She asks for help with the last crate. Physical teamwork opens the existing channel, and Duri carries the answering song around his delivery route. The imps tidy their props and join in. Roshan's voice belongs among everyone else's; the wider missing-candle plot stays open.

Rumi, Hana and Nari form the three-friend singing trio. Roshan remains the protagonist, joins their singing, notices the rescue, makes the decisive repair and returns to her familiar pool.

## Character candidates

- **Hana:** tallest of the trio; copper-red loose waves with a half-up twisted crown and one celadon shell clip, coral long-sleeved performance jacket over a cream high-neck blouse, celadon scales and deep-teal fan fin. Confident choreographer who learns to slow down and make space. No film ponytail, black graphic crop top, yellow mini-skirt, sword or copied markings.
- **Nari:** shorter and a little younger than Rumi/Hana; dark navy chin-length bob with curled ends and a silver crescent clip, mint hooded jacket, peach collar/scarf, lavender tail and pale peach fan fin. Funny, observant rhythm singer; first to follow Roshan's listening cue. No double buns, copied costume or weapons.

- **Duri:** existing lavender turtle with mint floral shell; a new supporting name and courier role, not a claim of earlier book canon.
- **Imps:** reuse existing purple glitter-jacket performer and navy-coated/top-hatted magician with trousers and shoes. Comical bubbles, soft props and bows; no injury or frightening weapons.
