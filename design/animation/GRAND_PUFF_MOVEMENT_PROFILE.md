Grand Puff's personality comes through broad cheek and mouth changes, unequal
laugh pulses, effort before aggression, coiled-ear follow-through and a warm
friendship settle. Preserve the contiguous lavender puff, paired coiled ears,
two ear-base pearls, two floor paws, crest and pale sparkle. No new anatomy.
The original videos supply individual changing poses; whole-image affine
movement of one still is not an adequate performance replacement.

The current three clips preserve all 145 frames each at 24 fps: laugh from
Grok R02, partial huff from R09, and friendship from R04. These came from ten
returns in the six-hour review cycle, not ten independent animation sets.

Laugh has happy facial pulses and ear/body movement; the source briefly hits
the top frame boundary. Huff has an initial smile, closed-eye cheek effort,
visible release and a determined finish; it is not a complete crouch/windup.
Friendship softens and shrinks, with source support drift retained. These known
limits are not silently repaired or accepted by the reconstruction.

All clips play once. The fixed canvas preserves source placement; source floor
motion remains observable. Stage navigation, jump/charge targeting, warning
flashes, dash, damage and saved progress stay owned by existing gameplay.
These standalone documents emit no gameplay events and change no interruption,
pause or save behavior. In-context contact and device costs must be measured
before any future integration. No new live integration is commissioned here.
