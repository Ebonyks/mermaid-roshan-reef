## Five-axis scorecard and knockouts

Score each axis in 0.25 increments. A knockout is an immediate
`REJECT_KNOCKOUT`, regardless of score. A non-knockout candidate below 4.5 on any
axis is `REJECT_SCORE` / `REGEN_REQUIRED`.

| Axis | Full-credit test |
|---|---|
| Style congruence | Polished flat 2D storybook language: broad matte bands, indigo/plum contours, aqua/lavender shadows, restrained wet accents; no 3D/PBR/photorealism/halo. |
| Scene congruence | Same cardinal north wall, water volume, coping, shelf/columns, scale and left/right compass as S01; no second room or theater plate. |
| Subject accuracy | Canonical V4 waterfall or seahorse silhouette/registration; Roshan context identity stable; seahorse plug topology and fixture contact exact. |
| Grime integration | Dirty material is opaque/stagnant and locally painted into water/stone with contact shadows; debris is embedded, not stickers or duplicate props. |
| Cinematic usefulness | One instantly legible focal defect at phone size, readable in a one-second hold, with clean hard-cut entry/exit and no cleanup/payoff implication. |

Immediate knockouts include: clean/flowing/glowing waterfall; any emitted seahorse
water; absent or mis-rooted mouth plug; generic/mirrored/moved fixture; wrong
Roshan or extra mermaid; 3D/PBR/photoreal or isolated product render; crop/layer/
cutout/translation/interpolation/duplicate-frame method; missing 16:9 native

Roshan identity unreadable at phone-size squint.
