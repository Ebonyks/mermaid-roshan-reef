## Required visual beats

1. **Dirty establishing frame:** dingy low-energy light. Pool contains six
   readable harmless trash pieces. Waterfall is rank and motionless. Seahorse
   is overgrown with the mouth obstruction clearly visible. Rumi is absent.
2. **Skimmer action:** Roshan sweeps the pearl-and-aqua skimmer beneath a piece
   of trash. Show unmistakable net/object contact, water resistance, a small
   wake, and a nearby shell basket receiving recovered litter.
3. **Waterfall action:** Roshan uses the shell scrubber on one of three vertical
   sludge lanes. Show a broad cleaned stripe beneath the tool while remaining
   lanes stay opaque and stagnant. The clean fixture is registered exactly to
   its original architecture; do not start the rainbow surge.
4. **Seahorse effort:** after the first two tasks, Roshan grips/pulls the mouth
   plug. Show the plug stretching away from the nozzle over several distinct
   effort frames, the sick seahorse reacting safely, and no injury or distress.
5. **Release/contact frame:** the wrapper/weed plug comes free and travels into
   the cleanup basket. The mouth is visibly clear; the seahorse begins to perk
   up. This contact must not be hidden by a hand, sparkle, crop, or motion blur.
6. **Reward frame:** only now does the room relight and the rainbow waterfall
   surge from top to basin. The seahorse fountain resumes clean water. Roshan
   reacts with delight.
7. **Rumi rise:** Rumi rises from the pool using her approved swim silhouette,
   thanks Roshan, introduces herself, waves, then settles into a calm idle.
