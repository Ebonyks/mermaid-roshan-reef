## Fixed room plan

The canonical north wall always keeps this west-to-east order:

```text
west service/steps -> clogged waterfall -> underwater window
                   -> sick seahorse -> east shelf/return

        one broad oval pool with shallow rear edge
west dry walk  <------------------------------->  east dry walk
        pearl coping and wide south promenade
                   south shell entrance
```

- One room, one oval pool, one waterfall, one seahorse, one window, one shelf.
- North/back is the fixture wall; south/front is the main entrance/prom.
- West steps descend through broad shell treads into the shallow basin.
- Curved west/east wall returns and a shallow radial shell vault close the room.
- Dry service/storage recesses remain subordinate beside the west steps and
  east shelf; never invent a second doorway or focal prop.
- Depth is expressed in flattened 2D through coping overlap, waterline,
  columns, pedestal contact, converging floor joints, local ripples, near/far
  scale, and rear-to-front water values. No 3D/PBR/theater construction.

## Non-negotiable dirty state

- Pool water is dingy murky blue-green/olive with broad algae clouds, scum
  flecks, muted reflections, and submerged trash edges. It is never pristine
  cyan or a black stage floor.
- Waterfall is an opaque olive-brown, turgid, rank, fully stagnant clog. No
  rainbow, cyan stream, foam, glow, clean stripe, spray, or sparkle.
- Seahorse is the exact blue/turquoise V4 fixture: lavender-pink long circular
  snout, purple scalloped crest/fin, peach belly, curled tail, algae/coral, and
  layered pearl-stone pedestal. Never cream, gray, horned, ceramic, or generic.
- Whenever its mouth is visible, a soggy **bright-pink folded wrapper plus
  olive seaweed** is rooted behind the nozzle lip. The fixture emits zero
  water. A pink-only cone, green-only clump, detached flower/ring/cup, or clean
  stream fails.
- Six pool objects keep stable identities: pink star wrapper, dented can, blue
  lid, one orange leaf, purple strip, yellow sponge. The mouth plug is separate.
- Roshan retains the approved child identity: tiara, chestnut hair with
  front-left rainbow ponytail/forelock, pink/lavender top, one continuous
  lavender/aqua rainbow-scaled tail, rainbow fins, stable face and anatomy.
- No basket, skimmer, tool, pointer, UI, target ring, Rumi, extra mermaid,
  cleanup, magic, clean fixture, reward light, or rainbow surge.
