# Day One cinematic local production plan — 2026-08-30

## Decision

Day One needs **13 discrete movies comprising 70 separately generated shots**.
The runtime should never ask one film to cover arrival, discovery, gameplay,
and restoration. The architecture is:

1. establish Roshan, Daddy, the plane, lagoon, and castle;
2. enter the castle and discover the overall problem;
3. for each of the four required rooms, play a short **dirty entry/discovery
   movie**, then gameplay, then a separate **restoration/relationship movie**;
4. reveal Grand Puff only after all four rooms are clean; and
5. close Day One with a clean-room montage and Roshan/Daddy reunion.

This makes “walking into a dirty room” and “seeing the room become clean” the
repeated cinematic grammar of Day One. The two movies bracket interaction and
make the child’s work, not an off-screen edit, the cause of the transformation.

`design/day_one_cinematic_slate_2026-08-30.json`. The existing-media evidence
is `audit/CINEMATIC_MEDIA_INVENTORY_2026-08-30.md`.

## Required beat plans

### D1-C00 — Opening Flight

Six jobs: plane in open sky; cabin two-shot; Roshan notices the new world;
Daddy reassures her with correct hand contact; Sky Lagoon/castle reveal; both
prepare to land. No otter, invented wildlife subplot, generic king, crown
change, airplane redesign, or geography jump.

### D1-C01 — Landing and approach

Four jobs: plane settles at dock; Daddy exits and offers his hand; Roshan and
Daddy travel together through the real lagoon geography; castle doors fill the
frame. The ending must inherit directly into C02. “Walking toward a castle” is
not enough; the sequence must establish the door and its opening direction.

### D1-C02 — first dirty discovery

Four jobs: exterior door opens inward; reverse from the dirty Main Hall toward
Roshan/Daddy; close inspection of safe grime/dust-bunny evidence; wide hall
orientation with only the Bathroom route glowing. This is the master example
of the dirty-entry grammar before room-specific films repeat it.

### D1-C03/C04 — Bathroom pair

Entry: dirty empty-room lock; Roshan crosses the threshold; dirty tub and
struggling swimmer closeup; sink/tools reaction and intent. The room must be
dim enough to read as neglected while remaining safe and friendly.

Restoration: last physical scrub contact; local water clears; swimmer recovers
and exits safely; clean wide settle with Roshan’s gaze toward the Pool route.
Frame zero must match gameplay’s final dirty state and the ending must match
the clean runtime plate.

### D1-C05/C06 — Pool pair

Discovery: doorway giant-pool orientation; low waterline trash; skimmer debris;
rank/turgid waterfall closeup; sick seahorse with visible pink trash in its
mouth; final wide problem map. The waterfall is stopped or sluggish and dirty,
never a bright flowing rainbow in the background.

Restoration uses the accepted nine-beat causal order:

1. Roshan pulls the pink trash from the seahorse mouth.
2. The seahorse produces the first clean flow.
3. The rainbow waterfall ignites **from the top downward**.
4. Camera pulls back so the rainbow water visibly enters the **giant pool**.
5. Seahorse and waterfall purification fronts meet.
6. The whole water volume clears and violet light rises from depth.
7. Correct Rumi/Violet emerges.
8. Rumi thanks Roshan.
9. Rumi gives Roshan a full, topology-correct hug.

The waterfall and seahorse both purify the water. Flow may never start at the
bottom, run backward, or terminate in a small invented fountain basin.

### D1-C07/C08 — Stuffie Room pair

Discovery retains the approved sequence: dirty-room orientation; Roshan enters;
reaction reverse; swinging dust bunny; gaze to floor; pinned Baby Eagle reveal.

Restoration retains: dirty calm; left basket warning; right basket warning;
four bunnies emerge; Baby Eagle sets wings; safe wing blast reveals the clean
room; clean resolve. Keep exactly one Baby Eagle and preserve the established
room geography across every angle.

### D1-C09/C10 — Art Room pair

Before any Grok job, author a clean and dirty six-view topology set: front,
left-doorway, right-doorway, low center, reverse toward entrance, and desk
closeup. The current front room plate does not show enough of the room to
support rotations; new boards must extrapolate a coherent full room rather
than paste clip-art props over the front view.

Discovery jobs: clean-topology orientation with dirty state; threshold entry;
four loose supply groups; three counter grime zones; Roshan resolves to help.
Use the current runtime prop identities—loose brushes, pink paint, blue paint,
paint cups, and three lavender paint-grime smears.

Restoration jobs: last grime clears; supplies travel to their established
homes; clean-room reveal; magic desk wakes; Roshan reaches for the unlocked
customization activity. Do not show a completed custom design before gameplay.

### D1-C11 — Grand Puff reveal

Four jobs: restored room lights converge on the boss door; Roshan approaches;
door opens into the established arena; Grand Puff lands and gives the playful
vulnerability tell. He is enormous and puffy but cute, never smoke, menace,
injury, or a defeated corpse.

### D1-C12 — Day One finale

Generate six separate shots: clean Bathroom with recovered swimmer; giant clean
Pool with Rumi; clean Stuffie Room with Baby Eagle; clean Art Room and awakened
desk; befriended Grand Puff/dust bunnies; Main Hall Roshan/Daddy reunion. The
montage may cut between rooms, but no single Grok job may generate multiple
locations. Rumi remains in the pool; she is not silently moved to the hall.

## Discrete Grok handoff contract

Create these directories, one per movie:

```text
assets_src/cinematics/<handoff_id>/
  HANDOFF_PACKET.json
  IMAGINE_HANDOFF.json
  SHOT_PLAN.md
  handoff_art/
  storyboards/
  shots/<shot_id>/PROMPT.txt
  shots/<shot_id>/SHOT_PACKET.json
```

For every movie:

1. Build the archive packet with all actual approved references, dimensions,
   SHA-256 values, provenance, source roles, topology rules, and story board.
2. Build one V2 generator card per shot from
   `design/templates/IMAGINE_SHOT_CARD_V2.md`.
3. Bind only two to four images. One camera move maximum. End with `Sound:`.
4. State start state, action, physical cause, end state, immutable objects,
   cast count, anatomy locks, and rejection conditions.
5. Inherit the previous accepted shot’s end frame when continuity requires it.
6. Run `python tools/audit_imagine_handoff.py <packet> --require-ready`.
7. Commit and push the packet; replace branch URLs with immutable commit/tree
   and raw-manifest links; verify every remote asset resolves.
8. Report `ARCHIVE_COMPLETE`, `GENERATION_READY`, and `DELIVERY_ACCEPTED`
   separately. Grok output is motion/editorial reference until full-frame
   cinematic evidence passes `tools/audit_cinematic.py`.

## Production order

Use this order to minimize rework:

1. Lock the shared Roshan/Daddy/plane/lagoon/castle authorities.
2. Build C02 Main Hall dirty discovery; it sets the dirty-entry grammar.
3. Repair and migrate the Bathroom pair.
4. Migrate the Pool pair—the restoration packet becomes the reference example.
5. Finish/publish the Stuffie pair.
6. Generate the Art Room six-view topology set, then both boards and packets.
7. Rebuild C00/C01 using the now-stable exterior/character locks.
8. Build Grand Puff reveal.
9. Build the finale only after all clean end frames are accepted, so it can
   inherit them rather than reinvent every room.

This order makes the room pairs available to production first and prevents the
final montage from establishing incompatible versions of the same spaces.
