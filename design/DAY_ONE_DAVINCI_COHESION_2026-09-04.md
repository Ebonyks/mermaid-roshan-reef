## Draft boundary

- Packet manifests classify every project, timeline, and export as `EDITORIAL_REFERENCE_ONLY`; the MP4 filenames themselves are not burn-ins, and no pixel-burn-in files are present in this packet.
- Hard cuts only. No optical flow, frame interpolation, morphs, cross-dissolves, freeze/duplicate concealment, subject-only reframing, warping, masking, or compositing.
- Preserve originals. A timeline may trim and order clips; this does not make their frames accepted cinematic delivery.
- C00 may run 24–30 seconds. Room discovery/restoration drafts should be 8–15 seconds, choosing only the clearest causal beats. C13 actions should be 3–5 seconds each.
- Do not stretch a scene to include every available file. A short honest fragment is preferable to a long false-complete scene.
- Grok MP4s remain motion/editorial reference and must not be copied into runtime `assets/` as accepted cutscenes. Runtime may expose an explicit opt-in draft slot, but production playback waits for full-frame evidence and `tools/audit_cinematic.py`.

## Animation versus runtime decisions

Animation must fix identity/anatomy drift, wrong room topology, missing physical contact, wrong cast count, backward or uncaused water, invented props, and mismatched opening/ending frames. Runtime code cannot disguise these failures with fades, UI, camera moves, or extra effects.

Runtime should fix when the clip is coherent but the game boundary is wrong: trigger timing, exact dirty/clean state selection, input lock, pause/skip behavior, save/checkpoint ordering, resume behavior after interruption, audio ducking, and the cut back to player control. A brief hard cut through black is acceptable as transition plumbing; it is not permission to conceal a bad endpoint.

For C05/C06 specifically, the animation causal order is: remove mouth obstruction → seahorse clean flow → waterfall ignites top-down → pull back to giant pool → two fronts meet → full water clears/violet light rises → correct Rumi emerges → thanks → full topology-correct hug. Runtime owns starting the clip only after the matching cleanup state and returning control only at the exact clean-room state.

## Sound policy

Resolve drafts are video-only by default. Mute autogenerated Grok dialogue and do not let clip audio overlap game music or protected family voices. Source effects may be auditioned on a separate disabled track, and may be enabled only after listening approval and provenance review. Draft sound placement is not audio acceptance. Runtime keeps game music and voice priority, with deterministic ducking and no generated dialogue substitution.

## Integration gates

- 1280×720, 24 fps source presented correctly on the 1280×720 Canvas without subject-only scaling.
- One-finger pause/skip is available; skip and natural completion converge on the same deterministic state.
- Input is locked during playback and restored once, with no accidental room interaction under the movie.
- Save state is written at a recoverable boundary; interruption or process death cannot strand Day One.
- Mobile renderer capture and Lenovo Tab M11 playback sustain the project’s 30 fps target around the transition.
- Full-speed human review covers identity, topology, contact, pacing, sound, and child-safe affect.
- `ARCHIVE_COMPLETE`, `GENERATION_READY`, and `DELIVERY_ACCEPTED` remain separate claims. This work changes none of them to accepted by itself.
