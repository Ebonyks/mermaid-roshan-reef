1. Base Environment ([BASE_FRAME]):

- Utilize a watercolor environment featuring dotted borders and dual seafloor mounds as the foundational layer.

2. Asset Handling ([USER_ASSET]):

- Extract the asset exactly as provided with zero alterations to style or texture.

3. Spatial Restrictions:

- Restricted Zone: Keep the top 85% of the frame entirely clear of assets.

- Bilateral Anchoring: Assets must be placed exclusively on the slopes of the left or right sand mounds.

- Z-Axis Grounding: Obscure the bottom 10% of the asset with the mound's texture and apply a low-opacity drop shadow to simulate weight.

- Render native elements like coral, shells, and bubbles with high-contrast, vector-like precision.
- Maintain crisp paper grain texture and clear gradients without digital noise.

Overall Tone:

- Professional, technical, and precise.
