## Intended reading experience

Roshan sees Pearl Castle, enters a dirty interior, receives tools, and helps make its rooms usable again. Each cleaning action should have a visible before/action/after relationship. Helping then becomes personal: two dust bunnies trap Baby Eagle, Roshan frees him, and there is room to play. Making her own art gives a pause before the rumbling door. The final challenge needs all four helpers; a hidden rainbow friend emerges, lands, and is welcomed.

The foreground carries those facts. Full-art pages supply places, actions and reveals. Reduced pages isolate tools, characters or a clear action to give the reader a pause. Marginal bunnies provide a second discovery after the main event is understood. Text connects visible beats rather than carrying missing action by itself.

## Sequence audit: revisions to the production approach

1. **Action comprehension is the first priority.** Pages 6, 22, 23 and 26 have a larger image/text gap than their border treatment. Check those source gaps before polishing all backgrounds. Pages 5 and 11 also narrate action that the current still only partly establishes.
2. **Avoid an inventory sequence.** Pages 19–22 contain four consecutive reduced pages. The new border score varies composition, but the foreground must progress from relief to invitation to sorting to completion. If an existing full-art desk-cleaning frame is available for p22, assess switching p22 to full art. Do not switch merely to satisfy an alternating template.
3. **Protect suspense.** Pages 24–26 currently repeat reduced-page presentation. The revised lower score deliberately becomes one listener, an empty border at the reveal, then one crouching reaction. Fixing the missing main dodge is still required.
4. **Keep the waterfall and rescue causal.** Dirty pool → collected rubbish → partial clearing → rainbow waterfall → fountain clearing → clear pool. Chirp → two bunnies trapping Eagle → loosening → free Eagle. Borders must neither omit steps nor insert substitute events.
5. **Preserve the finale.** Four helpers → soap concealment → rainbow jump and casing collapse → canonical landing → reflection → invitation. Do not alter frames to accommodate page furniture.
