Purpose and fixed story

Roshan and Daddy explore the dirty castle. Roshan cleans rooms, frees Rumi and Baby Eagle, and helps the little dust bunnies play gently. The friends wash uncomfortable Grand Puff until his own rainbow colors shine. Everyone rests together in the bubbles.

Rumi has lived in Pearl Castle for hundreds of years; the story does not say she was trapped for that duration. Exactly two playful dust bunnies trap Eagle accidentally. Puff receives care from the friends. The story and 34-page structure are finished; recommendations should refine clarity and expression while preserving those facts.

The intended experience is shared reading with growing participation through familiar phrases, picture retelling and recognizable words. A child may join “I’ll help!”, “Scoop, scoop!” or “Scrub, scrub!” without needing to read every caption independently.

Operational editorial rubric for this revision

These checks apply to the finished illustrated story in shared reading. They are project review criteria informed by the guidance below, rather than clinical norms or a screening test.

  1. Sequence: identify who needs help, what the problem is, the helping action and the result from the caption and picture together.
  2. Reference: identify each speaker and pronoun. Keep Rumi's long residence distinct from her present trap and preserve one continuing Grand Puff.
  3. Meaning: support new vocabulary and the rainbow metaphor with visible actions or context; preserve natural grammatical phrasing.
  4. Social resolution: distinguish playful intent from the accidental trap, then show apology and gentle-play guidance. Puff receives cooperative care.
  5. Participation: preserve voluntary echo phrases and room for discussion without requiring a child to answer or read independently.
  6. Typography: inspect native Sniglet 18–20 pt at actual 7 × 5 inch trim with 1.3× leading. Require unclipped type, phrase-preserving lines, quiet space and readable contrast.
  7. Art agreement: identify the named cup, visible fountain result, rainbow-water progression and two-bunny rescue. Marginal details remain secondary.
  8. Pacing: preserve clear problem/action/payoff episodes, the concealed Puff rainbow reveal and the gentle play-to-sleep ending.

Optional adult reading prompts

These are conversation ideas for the adult, separate from the child's story pages. Use a few naturally, allow time for a reply, and follow the child's interest.

  • After Rumi's rescue: “What was keeping Rumi stuck? What helped her get free?”
  • After the bunnies apologize: “How can the bunnies play so Baby Eagle feels comfortable?”
  • During Puff's cleaning: “What is each friend doing to help?”
  • At the end: “Tell me one part where Roshan helped someone.”
  • On another reading, invite the child to join one familiar sound phrase or tell a favorite part from its picture.
