# Character consistency fixes for V36

Status: `SUPPORTING_CURRENT` addendum to the V34 audit work order, written
2026-10-19 by Claude, who made no images. Codex builds every image. Page
numbers are V36 story numbers. Where V34 numbers a page differently, the V34
page is in brackets.

Roshan changes most from page to page: her rainbow lock, hair length and
colour, her tiara, one ear and her cover outfit. Rumi's face turns younger on
28–29, and the two dust bunnies that trap Baby Eagle lose their tall ears on
20. Daddy, Baby Eagle, Grand Puff, Lamma and the other little puffs stay
consistent. Fixes C1–C14 below are small local edits to existing art; none
redraws a scene.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-19)

Asked on 2026-10-19, the owner chose these two options:

- **Hair look: "Same rainbow lock."** "Every page gets the same rainbow lock:
  a pastel ponytail on her left side, about a quarter of her hair. Brown soft
  waves and a gold tiara throughout. Length may run shoulder to mid-back;
  waist-length hair is trimmed only where it can be repainted cleanly."
- **Locked art: "Yes, character-only edits."** "Allow small local edits to
  those characters only. The staging, action and composition of the cover,
  jump and landing stay exactly as they are." This covers the front cover and
  stories 28–29, which earlier rounds kept exact.

Earlier the same day the owner settled Roshan's ears as rounded (see
[the decision record](OWNER_DECISION_2026-10-19.md#roshans-ears-q6)).

## What holds and what drifts

- **Roshan** keeps her tail, the interior top, her palette and the gold tiara
  on most pages. Her rainbow lock drifts on 7 story pages and the rear cover,
  her tiara on 7 story pages and the rear cover, and her ear on story 3 and
  the rear cover. Her hair runs to her waist on 5 story pages and the rear
  cover. Her hair colour drifts on 3 pages, her curls on 2 and her face age
  on 1. The cover gives her a different top.
- **Daddy** matches on every page. His hair reads greener on story 3, the
  rear cover and story 32.
- **Rumi** matches except for her face on 28–29 (rounder, with big round
  pink eyes, younger than on 26–27) and her jacket on 17 (dark navy).
- **Baby Eagle** matches on every page.
- **Grand Puff** matches in both states. He is darker on 24 than on 26, which
  reads as the washing working.
- **Little puffs** match except the two Eagle bunnies on 20 (short curls
  instead of tall spiral ears) and the bath bunny on 7 (bright violet).
- **Lamma** matches on story 10 and the rear cover.

## Differences that stay

- Roshan looks taller and slimmer on 3, 11, 19 and 20 than on 26–29 and 32,
  and smaller beside Daddy on 26–29 than on 3–4. Changing body proportions or
  scale would mean redrawing the figure, which DL-ASSET-08 does not allow.
- Expressions change with the story: Daddy's eyes open or closed, Grand Puff
  lightening as he is washed. These are intended.
- Where her hair is not otherwise edited (the cover, 18 and 31), the rainbow
  lock stays brighter than the anchor's pastel. Its shape and side already
  match. The owner can ask for these to be repainted too.

## Method for every fix

- Edit the V34 art listed under References, after the V36 text and layout
  work and before any print upscale.
- One bounded polygon per region, nothing changed outside it. Record each
  polygon, source hash and output hash, and add an `ASSET_LICENSES.md` line.
- Bind `roshan_base.png` plus the page's own art. For side and back views add
  `roshan_directional.png`. For the lock, add story 31's V34 cutout as the
  in-book example.
- Start with two takes per fix. If neither passes, find out why before trying
  again.
- Check every edit at 100% for seams, halos and mismatched texture. A visible
  join fails: keep the original, record the gap and show the owner the best
  take.
- Build story 3 and rear cover A from one corrected derivative carrying C1,
  C2, C3, C7 and any C14, plus the rear's Lamma cameo.
- Never mirror a character. Never change a pose, contact, hands or staging.
  No caption changes.
- When done, review every changed page against the standards table. Then
  check spreads 8–9, 19–20 and 24–25, and the run 26–29, side by side.
