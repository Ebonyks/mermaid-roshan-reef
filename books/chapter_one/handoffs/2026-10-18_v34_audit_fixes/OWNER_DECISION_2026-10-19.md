## Print edition (replaces W16)

Owner direction: the final book is at least 8 × 11 in, possibly 11 × 14 in,
and art is upsampled only after the illustration fixes.

1. Finish and accept every illustration fix at the 14 × 10 in review size
   first. Do not upsample before that.
2. The owner picks one landscape trim: 11 × 8 in (the "8 × 11"; use
   11 × 8.5 in if US Letter was meant) or 14 × 11 in.
3. The art is 7:5. At 11 in wide it needs about 1.8% more height for
   11 × 8 in, 8% for 11 × 8.5 in, and 10% for 14 × 11 in. Add that height by
   outpainting above and below; never crop faces, hands, tails or the action.
   On cutout pages, extend the blue stationery.
4. Add 0.125 in of outpainted bleed on every edge. Set the PDF MediaBox to
   the trim plus bleed, with TrimBox and BleedBox present.
5. Upscale raster art to at least 300 ppi at trim plus bleed:
   3375 × 2475 px for 11 × 8 in, 3375 × 2625 px for 11 × 8.5 in, and
   4275 × 3375 px for 14 × 11 in. That is 2.3 to 2.9 times the 1484 px
   source. Use one recorded upscaler and one set of settings for the whole
   book. Compare faces, eyes, hands, tails and line work at 100% against the
   source and reject any result that changes identity. Keep the originals and
   record every hash.
6. Scale the text with the page: 20 pt body and a 24 pt inset on the 7 in
   grid become about 31 pt and 38 pt at 11 in wide, and 40 pt and 48 pt at
   14 in wide. Re-run the contrast gate on the composed art.
7. Keep key detail at least 0.25 in inside the trim. Story 1's tiara touches
   the top edge today; the added height gives it room.
8. **Accept when:** the boxes are present, every raster meets 300 ppi, the
   contrast gate shows zero FAIL, and the owner has reviewed a physical test
   print.
