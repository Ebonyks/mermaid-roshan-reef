and a caption split into two colours on 16. Two newer decisions needed the
owner: the new rear cover dropped the hidden Lamma, and the new page 9 drawing
is below print size. None of the fixes needs a scene redrawn.

## Method and limits

All 34 V37 page proofs were viewed one by one, with spreads taken from the
print pagination. The 125 MB lossless master (SHA-256 `dbe94612…a02a5c`) is
the file V37's verification binds to these proofs; it was too large to render
directly in the review environment.

The targets were the 2026-10-19 decision record, character standards C1–C14
and work items W1–W17 in the
[2026-10-18 handoff](../2026-10-18_v34_audit_fixes/START_HERE.md), plus the
V33 audit's ten fixes. Line positions come from V37's page provenance. The
cover subtitle's clearance from the ponytail was measured from colour hues
beside its first letters. Page 16's options were measured on its source art,
which has no local repaints.

V37 measured caption contrast on the final composed pages without the text,
including its local repaints: 0 of 85 lines fail and 6 need an outline.
Re-running the 2026-10-18 handoff's own script flagged 5 lines, but that
script ignores local repaints. Three of the five sit over V37's caption
repaints on pages 7 and 14. The other two, on pages 11 and 13, differ only at
the 3:1 threshold (2.97 to 3.08). V37's method is the more accurate one and
becomes the V38 gate.

This review does not establish owner acceptance, a child's response, a
speech-language review or a physical print test; V37's own records list all
four as open. One reviewer, at screen proof size.
