## Method and limits

The PDF the owner sent (SHA-256 `12e1f9b0…773b26`) matches V39's verified
lossless master byte for byte. Comparing page hashes showed 11 pages changed
since V37, five of them since V38. All 11 proofs were viewed, with Codex's
existing close-ups of the cover tails, cover Lamma, window portrait, sick
bunnies, door dust and page 2 landing, plus demo frame 408 and the Sky Lagoon
panorama. The other 23 pages are byte for byte the same as V37, so their
verdicts carry over.

Page 5's wisps were measured as the pixels that differ from V37 (2,326 pixels,
average contrast 1.44:1 with the door beneath). The stretch factors use each
source's size and placement from V39's page provenance. Caption contrast is
V39's own composed check: 79 lines pass, 7 need an outline and have one, 0
fail.

This review does not establish the owner's acceptance, a child's response, a
speech-language review or a physical print test. One reviewer, at screen proof
size.
