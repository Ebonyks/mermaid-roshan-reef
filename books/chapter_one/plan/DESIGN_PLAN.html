What changes

Cut around actions and meaningful objects, not screenshot rectangles. Alternate blue vignette pages with unframed full-art scenes. Compose wide reveals across actual facing pages. Keep short Sniglet captions in quiet art without opaque caption bars.

The sample changes its lower border for gardening, cooking, crafts and pets. This book carries that approach into baths, pool cleaning, rescue and art-making. Each prop belongs in the main action or the border, never both.

The blue family

These are prior blank studies, not yet compliant production backgrounds. New integrations preserve exact supplied assets, fit entirely below 85% page height, stay within 12% width/height, and sit partly behind mound texture. Large story cutouts belong to the separate foreground layer. Craft with cups is reserved for foregrounds without cups; marks-only avoids repetition. All lower panels stay blue.
