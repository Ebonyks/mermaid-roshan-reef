# Opera House — four-screen exploration v1

Status: `REVIEW_PROTOTYPE`; owner commission 2026-10-31. This plan and the
playable review are separate from production integration and acceptance.

## Intended experience

Roshan explores an opera house with four individually illustrated spaces in a
2 × 2 arrangement. A touch chooses a destination; her authored swim animation
carries her along a safe floor route before a doorway or stair activates.
The original coral, ivory, gold and teal architecture remains recognizable.

| Northwest | Northeast |
|---|---|
| Costume gallery: costume rail, mirror, hat boxes; Ballerina door | Rehearsal salon: theatre dais, barre, props; Magician door |
| **Southwest** | **Southeast** |
| Arrival lounge: shell fountain, conversation sofa; main entrance | Music gallery: grand piano, instruments, clock; Pop Star door |

These are four different drawings, not four crops or repeated instances of the
old foyer. The separate career crests sit above their physical door arches.
An earned star remains beside its crest after an activity, with no blanket
glow on every door. Existing Castle career ownership and Chapter Two story
gates remain production authority. The v1 uses the three resident Opera jobs;
it does not silently turn the venue into a new all-career story hub.

## Implementation sequence

1. **Review v1:** four new draft room drawings; independent doorway, crest,
   Roshan and chandelier cards; one-finger approach-before-entry; connected
   horizontal passages and paired stairs; a small read-only 2 × 2 locator.
   Use a brief room transition at a passage landing. This v1 demonstrates
   adjacency and travel, not accepted continuous panorama seams.
2. **Living space:** reuse the approved eight-state chandelier performance,
   localized soft curtain movement, sparse drifting motes and restrained
   light/water highlights. Architecture stays fixed. Roshan advances authored
   atlas cells while travelling. Pause/focus loss cancels a held gesture and
   freezes ambient motion. Keep the playable band visually quiet.
3. **Gameplay seam:** in the isolated review, enter the same shipping Canvas
   Ballerina, Pop Star and Magician games using their existing developer
   practice reward policy. Return to the same room and coordinate. A successful
   callback marks the corresponding physical crest for this review session;
   cancellation and passive observation never award it. No child save writes.
4. **After evaluation:** refine area sizes, door positions, motion amplitude
   and corridor/stair topology from the actual demo. Produce native background
   masters with at least 2048 × 2048 coverage for **each** playable screen,
   paint continuous joins, and slice losslessly to 1024 cards. Draft upscaling
   never counts as native coverage. Separate every interactive/occluding prop
   from its healed background exactly once. Register final navigation geometry.
5. **Production integration:** replace the normal venue through its existing
   lifecycle, preserve global and party-specific completion authority, saved
   masks, return routing and sparse retired slots. Run full local CI and exact
   topic-head CI before integration into dev. Check 1280 × 720 and wide phone,
   performance on M11, focus/pause/re-entry, save restore, child and owner review.

## Art and motion budget

Inventory: original July 21 foyer, existing career crest family, Roshan's
approved directional/swim atlases, existing chandelier animation sheet and
completion star. Those remain unchanged. Missing asset gap: four individually
drawn explorable spaces and a separate matching doorway; the old 1024 × 576
foyer cannot supply them. Initial built-in ImageGen allocation: one candidate
per drawing and one doorway. Retain raw files, prompts, dimensions and hashes;
all are draft candidates pending runtime owner review. No paid API job or new
character animation is commissioned by this allocation.

Only the visible room runs ambient decoration. Target fewer than 24 motes,
one chandelier, three depth roles, no new lights or 3D nodes, Mobile renderer
and 30 fps on the target device. Desktop measurements do not establish device
performance. Reuse authored character and fixture states, not whole-scene wobble.
