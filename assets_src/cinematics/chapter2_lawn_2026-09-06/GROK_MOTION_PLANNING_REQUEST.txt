OWNER-SELECTED STORY
Roshan completes the eight castle-day jobs and gathers her friends outside on
the Sky Lagoon lawn for her birthday. Every friend wears a party hat. She lights
the discovered rainbow candle using the little rocket she prepared. The Ember
King and his son arrive. The King demands the candle and challenges her. Roshan
expects him to be too strong, but successfully protects her friends across three
playable rounds. The King then cheats, takes only the candle and leaves with his
son. Roshan ends surrounded by her safe friends, ready to find their light again.
