INTERACTION CONTRACT
- Silent procedural UI fixtures, one cue at a time, 3.2 seconds of approach. Fixture order has no musical authority. Do not transfer it into an Iko Iko chart.
- The bar centre meets the outlined instrument target at cue arrival. Accept intentional pointer-down within +/-350 ms; target shape and colour must agree. No chords or held-note mechanics.
- Valid contact gives a 0.65-second warm ring on that instrument. Every owned touch receives a brief pale outline acknowledgement; wrong/early/late input adds no contact or progress. A missed cue leaves quietly and practice continues. There is no success threshold, score, punishment, theft trigger or reward in this lab.
- First-finger ownership lasts until release/cancellation. Holding early never turns into an automatic hit. Second fingers do not add hits. Exit and pause are always reachable.
- Focus loss pauses, clears held input and reveals resume. Resume provides at least 1.6 seconds of approach. Fixture reset has no game-save effect.
- The code deliberately does not play drums, move Roshan's sticks or fake contact through bobbing the whole cutout. The future on-contact seam must trigger a real approved strike/rebound at the corresponding visible surface.
