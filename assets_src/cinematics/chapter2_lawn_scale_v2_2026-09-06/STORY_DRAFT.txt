Status: rough story and alpha implementation draft. Owner direction in the
2026-09-05 Chapter 2 task selects the Sky Lagoon lawn, a protection victory
followed by the King's cheating theft, and a sincerely kind Prince conflicted
about his father. Character identity and cinematic delivery are separate reviews.

## The chapter's emotional promise

Roshan makes a birthday party with her friends. When someone stronger tries to
take it away, she discovers that she can protect the people she loves even when
she cannot stop him taking one precious thing. The child succeeds at every
playable objective. The King's theft is his choice, never a punishment for the
child's performance. Roshan gets to feel sad without being left helpless.

## From the castle to the lawn

Farmer Roshan brings five strawberries from the Sky Lagoon grove. Chef Roshan
mixes, bakes, stacks, and frosts the six rainbow tiers. Candy Maker Roshan glazes
those same five berries and places them on the cake. Painter Roshan finishes
the birthday banner. Ballerina Roshan teaches the stuffies their dance. Pop Star
Roshan and Rumi prepare the birthday song. Astronaut Roshan builds and parks a
little candle-lighting rocket. Finally, Detective Roshan finds the unlit rainbow
candle in the Royal Library's magic storybook.

The last finished job reveals the route outside. The Main Hall is a departure
point and preparation display; the celebration happens on the actual lawn of
the Sky Lagoon. No job or cake layer must be replayed to attend.

## Scene 1 — Everyone is here

The castle stands behind the lawn. The birthday banner hangs above the table.
The exact cake Roshan made rests at its centre, with the unlit rainbow candle
on top. Her little rocket waits beside it. Friends and stuffies gather in a
shallow semicircle, every guest wearing a party hat. Keep all approved friend
identities; hats are separate removable costume pieces, never painted over
protected portraits. Rumi's participation uses approved art and no invented
family voice.

Roshan looks from the berries to the banner to her friends. This is the visible
payoff for the minigames, not a checklist overlay.

**Roshan:** "We made all of this together!"

A friend straightens a crooked hat. The stuffies try their little bow. Roshan
raises a hand beside the rocket. The child touches the large rocket to begin.

**Roshan:** "Ready? Let's light our rainbow!"

The rocket gives the candle one small spark. The flame opens into rainbow
colours. Hold long enough to see that the candle is lit and everyone is safe.

## Scene 2 — Two unexpected guests

The Ember King arrives from the side of the lawn with his son. He is broad,
heavy, and certain the world should move aside for him. His son takes smaller
steps and notices the people before he notices the candle.

**Prince, quietly:** "You made that?"

**Roshan:** "All of us did. You can join us."

The Prince nearly smiles. The King steps between him and the invitation.

**King:** "A rainbow flame! That belongs at a KING'S party."

**Prince:** "Father, it's her birthday."

**King:** "Then she can show me how strong she is."

He plants himself on the open lawn and marks out the challenge with a heavy
foot. Roshan looks up at him, then back at the friends behind her.

**Roshan, softly:** "He's so big. I don't think I can beat him."

She moves in front of her friends.

**Roshan:** "But I can keep you safe."

The camera settles into the playable lawn composition. No surprise impact
crosses the cinematic-to-game seam; input starts at a quiet ready state.

## Scene 3 — Roshan protects the party

Use the boss bunny's readable cycle: a fixed painted warning, one-finger
movement out of it, then a large glowing target to tap during the opening.
There is no health bar, death, lost reward, or requirement to lose on purpose.

Three successful rounds put a protective rainbow around the guest group:

1. **Royal stomp.** One round warning locks on Roshan's previous position.
   She moves clear and taps the King's exposed glowing crest. The first part
   of the friends' rainbow shelter appears.
2. **Double demand.** Two separate stomps, each with its own full warning.
   The Prince points toward clear lawn: "Over here!" A successful counter
   grows the shelter. His help is real, not bait.
3. **Make way for the King.** One stomp followed by a clearly marked straight
   sweep. The King never changes a marked attack to chase the child. Roshan
   dodges and counters; the shelter closes around the friends and cake.

A bump briefly pauses the King, repeats the cue, and grants a longer warning.
A missed counter opens another chance. Finished rounds save immediately and
never reset. Waiting, held input, off-target taps, or hints cannot complete a
round. Counter timing becomes more forgiving after misses.

The King tries one final bluster against the finished shelter. It holds. The
friends wave their party hats. Roshan turns toward them, relieved.

**Roshan:** "You're safe!"

This is the battle's completed success. Save it before starting the next beat.

## Scene 4 — The King cheats

**King:** "Enough games. I am taking the light."

He reaches above the shelter with his royal magic and takes only the rainbow
candle. This is a deliberate new act after the challenge, not an attack the
child could have dodged. Show the intact cake and its now-empty candle socket
in the same composition as the candle in his hand. The flame stays lit.

**Prince:** "You promised a fair challenge."

**King:** "Come, son."

Roshan holds her place beside her friends. The Prince looks at his father, then
at her. He starts to speak, cannot quite do it, and follows his father.

**Prince, to Roshan:** "I'm sorry."

Both leave together. Do not leave the Prince behind as a permanent guest, and
do not imply that his warning made Roshan responsible for the theft.

## Scene 5 — What he could not take

Roshan looks at the empty candle place. Her friends close around her. One
offers her the party hat that slipped during the commotion. The cake, banner,
rocket, strawberries, music, and friends are still here.

**Roshan:** "He took our light."

She looks at the faces around her and steadies herself.

**Roshan:** "But you're all still here. We'll find it together."

End with company and a clear next route. Preserve the existing Chapter 3
unlock order; the eventual pursuit of the King must not bypass intervening
worlds. The lawn remains available as a safe completed celebration space.

## The two Ember personalities

**Ember King — bad cop, major antagonist.** His desire is recognition: the
brightest party, the biggest entrance, everyone looking at him. He mistakes
authority for ownership. Speak in short declarations: "Mine", "Make way",
"I said come". His theatrical indignation can be funny; his decisions still
hurt others and have consequences. He offers a challenge because he expects
easy admiration. When Roshan earns the real victory, he changes the terms.
No frightening injury, threats to kill, cruel taunts about Roshan's worth,
or suggestion that his behaviour is justified because he feels left out.

**Ember Prince — good cop, future love interest.** He genuinely wants to be
included and has learned to obey his father. He admires Roshan's resourcefulness
and courage, not her appearance. His hands open where his father's fists close;
his gaze returns to Roshan while his body follows the King. He warns her once,
objects to the theft, and leaves ashamed. Do not name romance in Chapter 2,
use hearts or a kiss, or reward him romantically for helping. Later affection
must follow his own repeated choices to make amends and oppose the King.

**Shared identity locks.** The owner's supplied `wybpcIGm.jpg` is the King's
highest authority and matches historical `ember_king_concept_v4.png`: stocky
red-scaled turtle-dragon, cream muzzle, amber eyes, obsidian crown and natural
shell with glowing orange seams, black spiky mane, charcoal armor and chain
belt, and enormous aubergine cape. Keep both eyes visible; do not import the
later V6 long fringe. The Prince uses the recovered approved identity from
commit `671b8b4a5315a6ce5e51a6f7b89ad35dda708fe5`: slender red/coral body,
asymmetrical black fringe, short obsidian horns, long split coat, ember-heart
clasp and compact anatomical shell. His height is 80% of the King's. Preserve
the exposed red skin around his shell and absent center-back coat panel.

Exact reference copies and hashes are in
`assets_src/cinematics/chapter2_lawn_2026-09-06/characters/REFERENCE_LOCK.json`.
The earlier volcanic guardian and squat Prince candidates were an assistant
