# Ember Prince — identity and continuity card

- Status: **APPROVED_PRIVATE_CANON** identity; motion package V2 is **REVIEW**.
- Stable ID: `ember_prince`
- Role: the King's observant son, initially accompanying the journey from
  ordinary family loyalty and habit rather than interest in the candle.
- Relative scale: his standing silhouette is exactly four-fifths (80%) of the
  Ember King's standing height. Preserve the ratio in every shared shot.

## Immutable traits

- Lanky young red/coral turtle-dragon with cream muzzle, short obsidian horns,
  amber eyes and asymmetrical black emo hair.
- Charcoal/aubergine sleeveless mall-punk jacket, ember-heart clasp, restrained
  plaid accent, slim dark trousers, boots, and long split coat tails.
- The compact vertically elongated shell grows from exposed coral-red back
  skin. A continuous red skin halo surrounds the entire shell.
- The jacket center-back panel is absent. No fabric exists beneath, behind,
  across or over the shell; coat tails begin below the opening and split around
  the visible red-skinned tail root.
- He does not want, watch, reach for or become fascinated by the candle.

## Acting and motion

His movement is narrow, diagonal, swift and economical. Eyes choose the path
first; feet arrive softly; the shell stays stable while hair, coat tails and
tail finish one restrained beat later. In family scenes he watches the King,
quietly clears hazards and protects his father's dignity without sharing the
goal.

His narrow build must not be enlarged to make him compete with the King's
mass. The 80% height lock distinguishes age and family hierarchy while his
faster posing, longer limbs and cleaner line of action preserve his sleekness.

## Attachment rule

Attach identity plus the accepted start frame for normal shots. Add the motion
authority for a sleek walk, evasive step or Cinderstep. For a rear or turning
shot, explicitly repeat the exposed-skin halo and absent center-back panel.
