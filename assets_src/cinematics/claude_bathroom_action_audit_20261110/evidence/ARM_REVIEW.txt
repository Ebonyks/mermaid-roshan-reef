Three reviewers traced the stiffness to two problems. The free arm
periodically bent against its resting elbow angle, crossing straight.
In stronger poses, the old forearm weights pinched across the painted
arm and the fringe beside the ponytail still followed the arm.

The new travel poses keep the normal elbow bend on both sides. The
free shoulder leads, the forearm stays folded longer as the stroke
reverses, and the hand unfolds into the glide. The brush arm keeps a
steadier bend. A targeted rebind spreads the right forearm influence
along the limb and reduces the pull at the left hair/arm fringe.
Original, Ballet and Magician share the same correction, one skeleton
and the same neutral shape. The nine original textures are byte
identical, and 292 anatomy vertices per look have changed weights.

REVISION DECISIONS
The first signed correction passed but still looked too straight.
The wider fold and broad recovery on the old mesh both failed strain.
Rebinding passed the Original quick test, then Ballet and Magician
exceeded 1.20 on their shoulder layer. The final candidate eases just
the rightward free shoulder in carry, preserving the reviewed elbow
and brush poses. A separate deeper left carry bend failed the 0.80
compression floor and was rejected. Exact failed samples are retained.
