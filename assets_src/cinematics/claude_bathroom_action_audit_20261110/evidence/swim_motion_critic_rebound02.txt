Findings
The baseline rightward free arm often makes a nearly straight line from shoulder to wrist. The repaired variants keep a visible bend, especially around .19 and .247, and the occupied arm holds the brush with its own steadier shape. In rebound02 the free-arm shoulder adjustment is small at all four recovery poses. The most visible differences from rebound01 are on the trailing free-arm contour. I see no material loss of elbow bend, visible shoulder seam, or visible shift of the brush and grip in these samples.

The final setting changes upper-arm compensation from .35 to .32 only for the free arm during rightward carry. The code applies elbow flexion and hand rotation separately. The two repaired candidates use the same bound rig. The leftward occupied silhouette reviewed on rebound01 remains a refinement opportunity outside this four-pose comparison.

Disposition and scope
Endorse rebound02 as a focused prototype improvement. These sampled frames establish pose readability and the visual scope of the final adjustment. Playback rhythm, arrival and departure continuity, unsampled poses, touch checks and the other costume skins are separate evidence. The parent was running the full verification at review time. This review used still frames and source and provides no device, child or owner acceptance.
