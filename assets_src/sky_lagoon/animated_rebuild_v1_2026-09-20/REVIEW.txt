SKY LAGOON ANIMATED CANDIDATE — REVIEW AND ROLLBACK

Status: opt-in gameplay candidate. Original art remains the default.
This package does not claim 4.9/5 visual, device, child, or owner acceptance.
It is layered Canvas2D gameplay, not cinematic delivery frames.

TRY IT
Use Godot 4.7.2-stable exactly. Open this work branch, then run:
  godot --path . -- --sky-lagoon-animated-preview
Enter Sky Lagoon through the normal game flow.
For a motion-free comparison of the same new artwork:
  godot --path . -- --sky-lagoon-animated-preview --sky-lagoon-animation-static
For original artwork and the original route, restart without either flag.
These flags do not migrate or replace save data.

ROLLBACK AND FAULT ISOLATION
The candidate preflight checks the whole manifest and all required textures
before applying any candidate art or route. Missing/malformed resources select
the original stage, instead of leaving a partly rebuilt scene.
For development, main.g owns these booleans (false disables the family):
  lagoon_environment_motion_enabled
  lagoon_plants_motion_enabled
  lagoon_huckleberry_motion_enabled
  lagoon_water_motion_enabled
  lagoon_bridge_motion_enabled
They survive level rebuilds in the current session. Change them through an
explicit debugging session; no player-facing settings or reading task added.
The CLI preview flag overrides lagoon_art_version. Remove the flag for a
complete original-version comparison. Teardown removes animation state and
restores the source background tile before rebuilding.

EDITABLE SOURCES
Open stage_masters/arrival-editable.ora, meadow-editable.ora, or
castle-editable.ora in Krita: 2048x2048 each, with 19/17/24 meaningful layers.
These are day/rest environment masters, not imported Krita animation timelines.
Original painted-in background masses still share a base layer. Foreground
flower, stem and branch components are separated where source geometry exists.
Do not assume the hidden backs of all plants have been reconstructed.

Use each family's .aseprite file for the runtime animation timeline:
  Grass: 4 frames; arrival native bough: 3 frames.
  Conifer: 6 frames; bellflower and huckleberry: 8 each.
  Bridge rigid planks and chains: 12 contact frames each.
  Water highlights: 12 frames; shoreline and tap ring: 8 each.
Bridge structure, plant roots and water geography remain fixed. No whole-castle
or whole-tree wobble substitutes for these owned motions.
TOOLCHAIN.json contains rebuild order and batch invocation details.

REVIEW IN MOTION
1. Arrival: inspect the lower tree bough at the left edge; reject a cut seam,
   halo, trunk movement or exposed fill. Check shoreline stays inside water.
2. Meadow: inspect grounded roots and planting hierarchy. Flowers should feel
   attached to stems and avoid repetitive synchronous movement.
3. Castle: tap the gate from the left approach. Roshan must walk across the
   bridge before entering; repeated taps must not teleport her. Near rails
   must occlude her correctly. Leave and confirm exact exterior return.
4. Pause each scene; resume without motion jumps. Compare day, night and static.
5. Tap water repeatedly: bounded rings should fade fully, remain in the pool,
   avoid opaque props and not keep an old walking destination active.
6. On the target device, measure 30 fps and texture/overdraw behavior. Desktop
   Mobile-renderer captures do not establish phone performance.

EVIDENCE AND LIMITS
stage_masters/VALIDATION.json: independent reopened layers and flat source
export reconstruct each master exactly.
evidence/source-rebuild.json: isolated batch rebuild reproduced all 16 derived
runtime textures pixel-for-pixel on the pinned local toolchain.
evidence/candidate-fallback-and-profiles.txt: whole-stage fallback/recovery,
resource fault injection, both original and animated L2 scene profiles.
PROGRESS.json: latest full regression state and outstanding acceptance.
The linked Grok return contains reports but omitted its MP4s/stills; no claim
is made to have reviewed or imported those clips. Local fallback cels are
independent, editable sources. No image-generation API key is required.
