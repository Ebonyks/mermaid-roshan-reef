# Sky Lagoon ambient-animal generation prompts

Mode: OpenAI built-in image generation. These are new project-original assets. File-reference ingestion was unavailable in the Windows sandbox, so the approved Sky Lagoon palette and shape language were carried in the text prompts. Each activation sheet used only the immediately preceding generated idle sheet as its identity/style reference.

## Hare idle

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: four-frame idle/wander sprite sheet for the Sky Lagoon promenade in a mobile children's game.
Primary request: Create exactly four consistent full-body poses of the SAME small summer-coat snowshoe hare, arranged as a precise 2-by-2 grid with no dividers: top-left calm standing profile, top-right nose-down sniff, bottom-left one gentle walking/hopping step, bottom-right alert glance with one ear turned. The hare faces screen-right in every pose and keeps identical identity, proportions, scale, baseline, lighting, and camera angle. Cute and gentle, recognizable hare anatomy, long ears, large hind feet, small tail.
Style/medium: premium polished hand-painted 2D children's storybook game sprite matching a cool high-key magical Pacific Northwest garden. Broad rounded slightly asymmetrical masses, two or three clear cel value bands, subtle soft painted texture, crisp deep plum/navy contour, oversized phone-readable features, no realistic fur strands, no 3D render.
Color palette: warm biscuit-brown and soft warm-grey body, cream muzzle and belly, coral-blush inner ears, lavender-grey shadows, dark plum outline. Warm foreground subject designed to contrast against cyan sky and emerald foliage.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat uniform solid #ff00ff chroma-key background across the entire canvas.
Composition: each pose centered within its own equal quadrant, generous padding, feet aligned to a consistent baseline, nothing touching a quadrant edge.
Constraints: one hare per quadrant, exactly four poses total, opaque subject, crisp separated silhouette, no cast shadow, no contact shadow, no floor plane, no scenery, no text, no labels, no borders, no icons, no clothing, no shell/rainbow/pearl decorations, no watermark. Do not use #ff00ff anywhere in the hare.
