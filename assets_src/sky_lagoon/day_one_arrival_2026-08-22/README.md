# Sky Lagoon Day One arrival landing

Gap: the approved Sky Lagoon v5 opening tile has a baked rope-and-stepping-stone
crossing underneath the Day One plane. The owner removed that bridge and asked
for the plane to read as grounded on dirt at the left of the opening frame.

`sky_lagoon_dirt_landing_v1_master.png` is the accepted transparent source made
with OpenAI built-in image generation on 2026-08-22. The input references were
the project-owned v5 lower-left panorama tile and one rejected full-tile edit.
No external art was used.

Final prompt:

> Create one large, irregular opaque dirt landing patch with soft painted
> grass-and-small-rock edges in the exact Sky Lagoon storybook style. It must
> completely cover the baked rope posts, ropes and stepping stones, provide
> solid quiet ground for the pearl airplane, and remain genuinely transparent
> outside the continuous patch. No bridge, water, airplane, characters, text,
> UI, frame, shadow, or watermark.

The runtime derivative
`assets/sprites/sky_lagoon/sky_lagoon_dirt_landing_v1.png` is a whole-canvas
Lanczos downsample from 1774x887 to 1024x512 with RGBA preserved. The source
master is retained here; the rejected full-tile edits are not runtime assets.
