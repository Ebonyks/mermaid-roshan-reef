## Non-generation processing

The installed ImageGen `remove_chroma_key.py` helper was run first with
corner-derived keying, soft matte, spill cleanup, one-pixel edge contraction,
and one-pixel feathering. Those `*_alpha_native.png` derivatives are preserved.
Global color dominance removed valid aqua water and coral wrap pixels inside
some subjects, so they are review evidence, not the final alpha authority.

`tools/prepare_opera_arborist_art.py` then used a deterministic
edge-connected-key matte. Only key-color regions connected to the outer canvas
were removed; accepted subject interiors were preserved. The helper result was
borrowed only for a narrow exterior despilled edge shell. The two dense tree
boards additionally clear pixels whose maximum RGB-channel distance from the
sampled magenta key is at most 96; that strict key-only rule is reasserted after
helper-edge borrowing to remove enclosed field islands without broad
color-dominance damage. Complete sheet cells were fit without repainting into
runtime cards. Each clean environment source remains preserved untouched in
this packet; its native-scale image was centered in the active band of a
2048x2048 whole-frame continuation with a uniform 24-pixel outer-edge feather,
then split into exact non-overlapping 1024x1024 tiles. Exact operations and
hashes are in `PROVENANCE.json` and `DERIVATION_REPORT.json`.
