MERMAID ROSHAN AND THE SONG BENEATH THE POOL
Owner-directed iterative 32-page review book, 2026-10-10.

This version revises only pages15-17: emotional bathhouse recovery, couch
downtime, and a funny interruption by the existing costumed imps. It reuses
the earlier bathhouse establishing art and all other selected book panels.
The two previous books and their native sources remain preserved.

All events remain beneath Rumi's swimming pool inside Pearl Castle. The
magic already exists. Roshan is the protagonist; Rumi/Hana/Nari are the
singing trio. Duri is rescued and later helps. No Kareem, candle recovery,
new magic portal, copied film characters, lyrics, weapons or lore.

The broad inspiration is private downtime for performers, affectionate
teasing, a shared snack, an unwelcome showy entrance and coordinated friends.
The newly authored staging, dialogue, costume identities and setting are
original. The owner specifically commissions these missing new-book moments;
protected artwork for the earlier Mermaid Roshan book is not redrawn.

comic_script.json is the page/panel/text authority for this candidate.
build_comic.py builds the PDF and records exact source hashes, normalized
crops, embedded fonts and declared JPEG95/subsampling0/no-resize embedding.
Native generator outputs are copied byte-identically into native/; originals
and every attempt are preserved and declared in generation_manifest.json.
art_jobs.json records the exact prompts and role-bound identity/style inputs.
verification/ITERATION_REVIEW.json records observation, hypothesis, changes,
native/page review, corrections, source gaps and outstanding acceptance.

This is a source-only reading/review candidate, not print production, runtime
integration, cinematic delivery, external animation handoff or release.
Agent visual review and machine checks do not grant owner or child acceptance.
Game-wide master remains UNSATISFIED; no finding is repaired or closed here.

Rebuild with bundled Python: python -B build_comic.py
The assets_src/.gdignore file excludes these source-book files from Godot.
