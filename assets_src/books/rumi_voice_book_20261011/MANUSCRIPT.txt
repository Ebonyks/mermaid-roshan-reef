PAGE 01 - Mermaid Roshan: Here I Am!

[p01.1]
Title: Mermaid Roshan: Here I Am!
Subtitle: An adventure beneath Rumi's pool

PAGE 02 - A turn of her own

[p02.1]
Narrator: The castle candle was still missing. But tonight, a song was waiting beneath Rumi's pool.

[p02.2]
Roshan: I want to sing the first line. All by myself.

[p02.3]
Rumi: Then let's make that your turn.
Roshan: What if my voice wobbles?
Rumi: It can wobble. It's still yours. I'll be beside you, if you want.

PAGE 03 - The neighbourhood song

[p03.1]
Narrator: Under the pool lay a whole neighbourhood. Tonight, everyone would sing together.

[p03.2]
Narrator: The baker packed warm snacks. The tailor hung bright ribbons. Everyone had a song to bring.
Rumi: The lanterns go up. The singing begins.

[p03.3]
Rumi: Stay beside me under the bridge. The tram stirs a strong current.

[p04.3]
