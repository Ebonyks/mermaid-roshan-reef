DUNGEON REUSE REVIEW — 2026-09-06

Active consumers are confirmed in scripts/dungeon_level.gd (10 castle rooms),
scripts/ember_fortress.gd (6 Ember rooms), scripts/dungeon_puzzle_room.gd and
scripts/combat_arena.gd. Both theme dictionaries in scripts/dungeon_art.gd
have empty PATHS/EMBER_PATHS, so every listed room currently reaches the
crossed-card fallback. This is an active shared defect, not speculative room
redesign.

The three safest existing semantic matches are conditional reuse candidates:
cleanup_basket.png for Basket, moonflower_door_closed.png for Door, and
dusty_attic_arena_2048.png for a full Arena background. Each has a full hash
and dimensions in dungeon_reuse_evidence.json. Their caveats are explicit:
the basket and door are richly ornamented, and the arena is a full background
rather than a small crossed-card part. None should be bound blindly.

The QA lantern and river-stone images are opaque low-poly review cards and are
rejected for direct runtime binding. sky_lagoon_castle_door_focus_v1.png is
all-white opaque and rejected. Existing gen2 Image_0 files are raw UV maps and
must not be presented as finished role cards. Pictogram symbols remain valid
functional child stimuli; simple primitive construction alone is not a defect.

No script, runtime asset, canonical asset, or protected original was edited.
