   Root has since closed this defect in snapshot 022ce0ba/rootcoverage; the

   garden_watering_can_v3.png (hash prefix 98b47563). Verify that exact

   and never edit assets/book/ or a protected derivative.
2. PictureGames garden currently mixes clean flower/sprout cards with that
   crop; flowers are readable but use soft gradient treatment, while the
   watering can breaks the transparent-card contract. Repair the can first.
3. PictureGames snowman/xmas uses oversized highly textured painterly cards
   (xtree/ornaments) against generated ColorRect/gradient surfaces. At child
   scale the tree/ornaments read, but their grainy/high-gloss treatment and
   jewel accents conflict with the new clean ink / opaque plane / 2–3 shadow
   shape direction. Bounded replacement should target only tree + ornament
   cards after reuse check; primitive circles are not themselves defects.
4. Pool cleanup visuals (floating_trash_atlas, pool_skimmer,
   waterfall_clogged_original_match, waterfall_scrubber, seahorse_sick_clear_mouth)
   are readable and alpha-backed, but heavily glossy, pearl/jewel-decorated and

   pass has high reach. Do not touch protected room/book originals.
5. Fairy Pond uses the 4096x1024 pond_panorama plus 1024 cards. The panorama has
   visible watercolor-like mottling and dense decorative sparkle; bug_jewel and
   helpful_flower_gate add jewel/ornament overload. The boss bloom has strong
   contour readability but the whole group needs a bounded style harmonization
   review before any regeneration.
6. DungeonArt PATHS and EMBER_PATHS are all empty; runtime therefore falls back
   to generic crossed cards (rock, shell, turtle, sand dollar, pine, carrot,
   star, etc.). This is the largest art-integrity risk: ten castle rooms plus
   six ember rooms inherit generic substitutes, while the logical puzzle icons
   remain readable. Repair needs role-specific 2D cards/backgrounds per shared
   role, preserving puzzle silhouettes and no-fail interaction geometry.
7. Fetch, Shop, CombatArena, StuffieBattle, KartGame and EmberFortress are
   largely procedural/3D presenters with authored sprites only for some actors.
   Their visual result remains UNVERIFIED until contextual screenshots are
   captured; source alone does not justify calling simple indicators defects.
