WORLD-FIT ACTIVITY ARTWORK REVIEW V2
Status: review-only inventory and proposal. No runtime replacement, canonical design
language update, master-audit entry, or license decision is made here.

Scope and choice
Three live Opera activities are used as a representative quality check: Painter,
Candymaker, and Ballerina. Their current scripts and art are the binding evidence.
The chooser study should make the child recognize a tangible action from the
picture itself. A crest or abstract mark can support identity, but cannot carry the
activity alone. Existing protected characters and world paintings remain references
and are not edited or regenerated by this review.

1. PAINTER
Live usage:
- scripts/chapter_two_career_scene_adapter.gd: PAINT uses `paint_reveal` with
  `widget_context: trace_painter`, and `STAMP`/`HANG` use `target_painter` and
  `lanes_painter`. All three phases act on
  `assets/flats/castle/logo_studio_v2/castle_banner_rainbow.png`.
- scripts/opera_gesture_surface.gd loads
  `assets/opera/worlds/props/goal_painter.png` as the completed paint-reveal art.
- `assets/opera/worlds/props/goal_painter.png` is a specific painted sunrise in a
  scalloped shell frame: a strong completion image, but it does not show the child
  painting the birthday banner.
- `assets/opera/worlds/widgets/widget_trace_painter.png` is a 1024x608 framed
  easel with a dark zigzag stroke. It communicates “draw” only through one broad
  line and a blank canvas; it is the clearest generic first-draft weakness.
- Non-UI world reference:
  `assets_src/imagegen/opera_codex_2026-08-02/native/stage_painter_native.png`
  shows the authored underwater opera stage, three paint tubs, easels, palettes,
  and broad painted ground.

Proposed chooser vignette: a close, readable easel with the actual rainbow birthday
banner, brush visibly crossing the banner, palette/paint cups, and one bright newly
painted star or sunrise patch. Keep the gesture and object relationship legible at
card scale. Do not reduce this to a paint blob, color swatch, or generic brush icon.

2. CANDYMAKER
Live usage:
- scripts/chapter_two_career_scene_adapter.gd binds `pourt`, `candy_sort`,
  `circle`, and final `tap` phases to strawberry, syrup, cake, tray, and
  `target_candymaker` contexts.
- `assets/opera/worlds/widgets/widget_pour_candymaker.png` is an unusually useful
  action source: a scallop mold, four colored candy jars, thermometer, wrapped
  sweets, ladle, coral, and pearl ornament. It has material and place cues, though
  its empty mold is still a widget backdrop rather than a complete activity card.
- `assets/opera/worlds/props/goal_candymaker.png` is a glossy wrapped red candy;
  good identity/completion prop, weak action picture by itself.
- `assets/chapter2/birthday/sky_lagoon_strawberry_single.png` and
  `assets/chapter2/birthday/chapter2_grand_five_strawberry_cake.png` are the actual
  ingredient and destination art for the final placement phase. The candied tray
  is `assets/chapter2/birthday/chapter2_candied_strawberries_tray.png`.
- Non-UI world reference:
  `assets_src/imagegen/opera_codex_2026-08-02/native/stage_candymaker_native.png`
  shows the candy workshop, three candy dispensers, chute/board, and handoff table.

Proposed chooser vignette: show a ladle tipping glossy syrup onto and coating one
  recognizable strawberry in the shell mold, with colored jars and the cake handoff
  visible behind it. The liquid-to-strawberry action must be obvious. Avoid an empty
  mold, a single recolored candy circle, or a candy-maker crest used as the entire
  picture.

3. BALLERINA
Live usage:
- scripts/chapter_two_career_scene_adapter.gd binds the `stuffie_room` scene to
  `ballet_pose`, `ballet_ribbon`, and `ballet_twirl`; its actual backdrop prop is
  `assets/flats/castle/rooms/room_playroom_item_stuffie_nook.png`.
- scripts/opera_ballet_surface.gd loads the actual
  `assets/opera/worlds/actors/animation/roshan_ballerina_sheet_a.png` and
  `assets/opera/worlds/props/goal_ballerina.png`; ribbon and twirl paths are drawn
  procedurally from the live touch geometry.
- `assets/opera/worlds/props/goal_ballerina.png` is a richly shaded shell music box
  with a treble clef: useful identity and stage prop, but not a dancer/action.
- `assets/opera/worlds/widgets/widget_trace_ballerina.png` is a 1024x608 framed
  rainbow ribbon with a broad dark line; it communicates a path but lacks dancer,
  stuffie, or music-box context. The dedicated `assets/opera/worlds/ui/ballet`
  folder currently contains only `.import` metadata, so those named source files
  are not safe references for a new board.
- Non-UI world references:
  `assets_src/imagegen/opera_codex_2026-08-02/native/stage_ballerina_native.png`
  shows the painted recital stage, pearl dance pads, disco balls, and audience;
  `assets/flats/castle/rooms/room_playroom_item_stuffie_nook.png` shows the actual
  soft stuffies in their shell nook.

Proposed chooser vignette: show Roshan and/or the soft stuffies following a pearl
along a colored ribbon on a small music-box/stage setting. The bend of the ribbon,
the dancer pose, and the companion relationship should be visible together. Avoid
an isolated rainbow stripe or a generic dance/music symbol.

Scoped flat-object defect shortlist
- scripts/games/picture_games.gd::_mg_build_slide constructs six 1500x60 rotated
  ColorRect bands (rotation 0.5) as the rainbow slide. This is a source-level
  quality concern: no painted material, contour, or contact shading is present.
- `assets/props/gen2/play_slide_Image_0_flat.png` is a flat-color/UV-style source
  and is not a standalone illustration. Do not use it as chooser art.
- `assets/sprites/sky_lagoon/sky_lagoon_slide_v3_compact.png` is a shaded authored
  slide card and is a good future repair/reference candidate; its use here would
  broaden scope beyond the three selected Opera cards.
- Simple `draw_circle` uses elsewhere are not blanket failures: inspect each for
  whether it is a small feedback mark or the primary activity object before a
  replacement decision.

Additional bounded quality candidates
- `assets/mg/flower.png` is a simple gradient petal-disk/stem object used by the
  garden activity (`scripts/games/picture_games.gd` around `_mg_build_garden`).
  It can read as an intentional child-readable silhouette, but currently lacks
  the painted material bands and contact detail expected of a hero activity prop.
- `assets/mg/wateringcan.png` is a low-resolution pink watering can with visible
  surrounding-background fragments. It is a specific object, yet its cleanup and
  material separation are weaker than the Opera props. Review as a scoped repair
  candidate only; do not alter any protected book crop or infer a blanket ban on
  simple silhouettes.

Shared review recommendation
Use a single modest painted shell/scallop frame family, with three equal large
activity cards and one neutral return/doorway control. Let each card carry rich
specific object/action detail, with restrained frame ornament and quiet interior
space. Preserve at least 110x110 base hit allocations in any future native
reconstruction; the generated board is review art and cannot prove hit sizes.
The three candidate actions are Paint the birthday banner, Pour syrup into the
scallop candy mold, and Guide the pearl ribbon while the stuffies dance. Human
review remains required before any runtime or canonical-art decision.
