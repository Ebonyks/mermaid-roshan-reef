HUMAN REVIEW — WORLD-FIT MENU REDESIGN
Status: PENDING_HUMAN_EVALUATION

This packet contains local, non-runtime concept images only. The next step is
human evaluation of the menus and the minigame picture quality. An internal
AI review may identify defects but does not approve this design for adoption.
No new design-language rules or findings are being added to the master audit
in this round. No runtime, protected artwork, save schema, commit or publication
change is part of this packet. Earlier-turn document drafts remain as they
were at this round's start; review_scope.json records their frozen hashes.

Review focus
- Does the simpler painted frame feel part of the existing game world?
- Are the activity objects carefully drawn: clear form, colored shadow,
  meaningful material details and clean edges, instead of flat-color blobs?
- Does each minigame picture communicate its actual activity without text?
- Is the amount of shell/pearl/rainbow detail comfortable, without becoming
  either ornate jewelry or a plain generic app panel?
- Are the choices easy to distinguish at whole-screen phone size?

Interpretation limits
The pause care basket is a proposed care-route picture, not an approved friend
portrait or evidence that a child recognizes the Stuffie route. The exit arch
needs the actual destination context in a live implementation. Activity cards
are illustrative review pictures, not screenshots or evidence of live input.
Artwork quality does not prove touch, voice, save, device or child behavior.

Current minigame-art inspection
The source of the picture-game rainbow slide uses six flat rotated ColorRect
bands (scripts/games/picture_games.gd, _mg_build_slide). This is a specific
source-level candidate for art repair, not a new runtime capture. A reusable
painted slide card already exists at
assets/sprites/sky_lagoon/sky_lagoon_slide_v3_compact.png, used by Sky Lagoon
and slide_race; a future adaptation must preserve the different game's path
and interaction. Do not substitute a legacy texture atlas for a usable card.
Luna's separate artwork review records other inspected candidates and limits.
