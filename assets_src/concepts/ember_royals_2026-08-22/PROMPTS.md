## V2 audit/design-language basis

The V2 correction follows the authority chain in
`audit/MASTER_AUDIT_2026-08-09.md` section 3: direct owner decisions, then the
canonical `design/06_COMPREHENSIVE_DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md`. In particular:

- `DL-AGE-08`: soften threat, glare, teeth, and scary imagery into playful,
  child-scale fantasy.
- `DL-MED-05`: preserve authored 2D contours, identity colors, and authored
  light without mesh-like relighting.

  silhouette, two or three calm detail clusters, clean plum/indigo contours,
  high-key values, lavender/blue-grey shadows, and broad painted value bands.

  one primary focus, and quiet peripheral treatment.
