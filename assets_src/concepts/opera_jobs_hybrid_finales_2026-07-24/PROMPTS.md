## Shared prompt contract

Create one polished wide flat-image prototype for a Mermaid Roshan Opera
career performance and friendly theater boss finale.

Binding requirements:

- one Mermaid Roshan in the accepted career outfit;
- one recurring floor boss;
- a formal rounded Opera proscenium and broad readable stage;
- the job's real implements and learned interaction, not a generic attack;
- coral, teal, cream, and plum palette with floor-specific emphasis;
- navy-purple outlines and aqua/lavender shadow shapes;
- restrained brushed gold, pearl, shell, rope, and footlight accents;
- flat storybook-cel finish with rounded toy-diorama forms;
- clear enough to reproduce as modular Mobile-renderer 3D;
- supportive performance tone, visible applause or footlight progress;
- no words, letters, numbers, logos, copied franchise imagery, photorealism,
  damage, health bars, fire, weapons, frightening attack, or defeated boss;
- boss and Roshan must be able to bow together after the final beat.

Use the established project-owned Roshan, Opera floor, outfit, implement, and
boss references as continuity inputs. Do not use external visual references.
