# Northern Promenade 2.5D environment sources

## Status

The eight 1024x576 files beside this document are preserved concept/layout
references. They passed the composition and style review, but they are not
accepted final runtime environment sources under the 2026-07-27 native-2K
amendment. They must never be enlarged, interpolated, AI-upscaled, or padded
to satisfy that amendment.

The current layered scene is an inspectable implementation concept built from
those files. Final wiring remains blocked until all eight exact-ratio,
native-long-edge >=2048 fidelity regenerations pass
`tools/validate_northern_2k_sources.py`.

## Original concept generation record

Generation mode: OpenAI built-in image generation, new image. The only image
references supplied to the generator were project-owned Northern Kingdom
concept and QA renders:

- `assets_src/concepts/northern_kingdom_quality_2026-07-19.png`
- selected renders from `assets_src/blender/qa_northern_kingdom_kit/`

The selected QA inputs were pre-existing flat PNG files only. No GLB, `.blend`
file, Blender process, or 3D authoring tool was opened or invoked.

No Disney, Frozen, Skyrim, Riverwood, video-frame, or other third-party image
was supplied. The named references in the design brief were researched only
to extract general composition principles: an enchanted autumn forest, a
single road organically dividing a riverside settlement, and a luminous
palace finale.

## Shared final prompt

Use case: game-environment background. Asset type: one opaque 16:9,
side-view 2.5D storybook game background for a preschool touch game. Match the
attached project-owned Northern Kingdom references: polished stylized
toy-diorama painting, rounded hand-crafted forms, navy/plum edge language,
pastel aqua and lavender shadows, berry and warm autumn foliage, cream paths,
teal water, and warm gold lights. Build a clear, uninterrupted walkable band
across the lower-middle of the picture, with layered foreground foliage,
midground play path, and atmospheric depth. Keep hazards decorative and the
route unmistakable for a non-reader. High authored detail in broad,
mobile-readable shapes; no characters, words, UI, logos, watermarks,
photorealism, copied franchise designs, franchise symbols, or text. Create an
original Mermaid Roshan location rather than reproducing any named reference.

## Native >=2K exact-aspect fidelity regeneration contract

Each pass must use the matching approved 1024x576 concept as its sole
composition reference and append its panel-specific description above to this
exact final instruction:

> Resolution/detail-fidelity regeneration only. Create a new raster natively
> with a long edge of at least 2048 pixels while preserving the attached
> approved concept's exact aspect ratio within one-pixel rounding tolerance.
> For this 1024x576 source, use 2048x1152 or a larger exact 16:9 size. The
> attached approved concept is immutable for
> composition: preserve its viewpoint, camera height, palette, lighting,
> object identities and shapes, object placement, silhouettes, negative
> space, navigation lane, interaction sockets, and foreground/midground/
> background depth boundaries. Add, remove, replace, crop, stretch, pad, or
> redesign nothing. Repaint the same scene with finer native detail and clean
> storybook edges. Do not enlarge, resample, interpolate, AI-upscale, or pad
> the supplied raster. Output one opaque RGB/RGBA landscape PNG with the exact
> approved aspect ratio, no text, UI, logo, watermark, characters, or
> franchise symbols.

Acceptance requires all of the following:

- the returned master has a native long edge of at least 2048 pixels;
- its aspect ratio matches the approved source within one-pixel rounding
  tolerance, with no crop, stretch, padding, canvas extension, or letterbox;
- the generator path/ID, final prompt, references, dimensions, and SHA-256 are
  recorded in `audit/northern_promenade_2k_manifest_2026-07-27.json`;
- every no-transform field is false for resize, interpolation, AI upscale, and
  padding;
- the recorded programmatic invariance metrics match a fresh validator run;
- the seven visual invariance checks are explicitly approved;
- the strict validator returns `NORTH2K|RESULT|OK`.

Adjacent unshaded Sprite3D cards reconstruct those tiles at one coherent depth.
